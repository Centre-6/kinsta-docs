# Dallas Data Center Is Now Available

**Product:** Changelog
**Source:** https://kinsta.com/changelog/google-cloud-dallas-data-center/
**Kinsta last updated:** 2026-09-16T09:49:41

---

We’re delighted to announce the addition of a new **Dallas**, Texas, location to Kinsta’s list of customer-accessible [Google Cloud Platform data centers](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/data-center-locations/)!

This new Dallas data center location is already **available on all plans for free**, along with our 30 other locations spread across the globe. By choosing a location close to their userbase, Kinsta customers can count on [significantly reduced latency](https://kinsta.com/blog/network-latency/), meaning [faster load times](https://kinsta.com/blog/ttfb/) for all their visitors.

If you’d like to set Dallas as your data center, just follow these steps in MyKinsta:

1. Navigate to your [MyKinsta dashboard](https://kinsta.com/mykinsta/), click on the **Sites** option on the left sidebar, and then **Add Site** to create a new site.
2. Once there, select **Dallas US (us-south1)** as your preferred location option from the dropdown menu.

![A screenshot of MyKinsta showing the "Add Site" location drop-down set to ](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/dallas-texas-gcp-data-center.png)

Host your WordPress site in Dallas, Texas.

All Kinsta-hosted sites enjoy the use of [Google Cloud Platform’s C2 VMs](https://kinsta.com/wordpress-hosting/) on top of the benefits of a [low-latency Premium Tier network](https://kinsta.com/blog/cloud-platform-for-developers/). Combined with a selection of 30 data center locations to choose from, Kinsta’s managed WordPress hosting is the easy choice for anyone seeking a [boost to their site’s speed](https://kinsta.com/blog/cloudflare-integration/).
