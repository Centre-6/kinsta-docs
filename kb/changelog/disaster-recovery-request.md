# It’s easier to get started with Kinsta Disaster Recovery

We’ve made it simpler for our dedicated-server customers to request, cancel, and manage disaster recovery protection for their valuable WordPress properties.

Launched in August 2026, [Kinsta Disaster Recovery](https://kinsta.com/changelog/disaster-recovery/) delivers continuous data replication between servers in different data centers, creating a mirrored environment ready to go live if a primary data center is knocked offline.

It’s not easy to take out an entire data center, but flooding, fire — even war — can do it. And now, Kinsta customers can prepare in advance by requesting disaster recovery protection in the [MyKinsta Dashboard](https://kinsta.com/mykinsta/).

## Beginning a Disaster Recovery request

Disaster Recovery is an add-on for our dedicated-server customers. To initialize a request for the service, navigate to **Sites** > ***sitename*** > **Add-ons**, scroll to **Disaster recovery**, and click the **Request add-on** button:

![A screenshot showing the Disaster Recovery card among the add-ons in MyKinsta.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/mykinsta-add-ons-disaster-recovery.png)

Requesting disaster recovery in MyKinsta.

In the example above, the Dedicated Server add-on is not already active (it’s still available on request). In such cases, a customer clicking the **Request add-on** button for Disaster Recovery will be told the site must first be moved to a dedicated server.

If the active site is on a dedicated server, you’ll be asked to confirm your request for Kinsta Disaster Recovery after reviewing its charges:

![A screenshot showing the confirmation dialog for Disaster Recovery.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/mykinsta-add-ons-disaster-recovery-confirmation.png)

Confirming a request for Disaster Recovery.

Clicking the **Request disaster recovery** button shown above will launch a chat with our Support team. We’ll take the next steps and alert you when the service is enabled.

## Making changes to Disaster Recovery

Later, you may want to modify or cancel your Disaster Recovery protection. Returning to **Sites** > ***sitename*** > **Add-ons** and scrolling to **Disaster recovery**, you’ll see a **Change** button:

![A screenshot of an add-on card within MyKinsta with the Change button highlighted.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/mykinsta-add-ons-disaster-recovery-change.png)

Starting a change request for Kinsta Disaster Recovery.

After clicking the **Change** button, tell us what modifications you have in mind. Then, clicking the **Send request** button will launch a chat with a Support team member who will have the details of your site’s configuration at hand:

![A screenshot showing the dialog for requesting Disaster Recovery changes within MyKinsta.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/mykinsta-add-ons-disaster-recovery-change-request.png)

You can describe your Disaster Recovery change request.

## Do you need a dedicated server for your WordPress site?

Kinsta’s Dedicated Servers offer exclusive access to a cloud device’s CPU and RAM to handle the toughest processing loads. Some of our customers deploy dedicated servers to handle traffic surges during recurring sales events or high-profile promotions. Others need that kind of dedicated power 24/7.

Currently, only customers with dedicated servers can take advantage of Kinsta Disaster Recovery protection. You can request a dedicated server for your important website by navigating to **Sites** > ***sitename*** > **Add-ons** >, scrolling to **Dedicated server**, and then clicking the **Request add-on** button.

Our team will work with you to configure the dedicated environment your site requires.
