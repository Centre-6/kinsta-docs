# Delhi Data Center Is Now Available

**Product:** Changelog
**Source:** https://kinsta.com/changelog/google-cloud-delhi-data-center/
**Kinsta last updated:** 2026-09-16T09:49:13

---

[Google Cloud Platform’s data center locations](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/data-center-locations/) keep growing, hence the possibilities for Kinsta customers to take advantage of new locations for their websites and online stores.

Following [last week’s announcement](https://kinsta.com/changelog/google-cloud-melbourne-data-center/), we’re thrilled to share the latest news with you all: **Delhi, India**, is now available as a new data center location to **all Kinsta customers,** **on all plans**.

Dehli’s data center is the second data center available in India and the eleventh location that serves the APAC region in total, joining:

- Mumbai (IN)
- Changhua County (TW)
- Hong Kong (HK)
- Tokyo (JP)
- Osaka (JP)
- Seoul (KR)
- Jurong West (SG)
- Jakarta (ID)
- Sydney (AU)
- Melbourne (AU)

Here’s how to set Delhi as your data center in MyKinsta.

![Set Delhi, India as your data center in MyKinsta](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/delhi-gcp-data-center.jpg-1024x611.jpg)

Host your WordPress site in Delhi, India.

Navigate to your [MyKinsta dashboard](https://kinsta.com/mykinsta/), click on the **Sites option** on the left sidebar, and then **Add Site** to create a new site.

Once there, select **Delhi (IN)** as your preferred location option from the dropdown menu.

That’s it!

Powered by [Google Cloud Platform’s C2 VMs](https://kinsta.com/wordpress-hosting/), [low-latency Premium Tier network](https://kinsta.com/blog/cloud-platform-for-developers/), and 30 data center locations to choose from, Kinsta is the right host for WordPress sites.
