Updated

![PHP 8.1](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/php-8-1-kinsta.jpg)

Every time a new version of PHP is released we all get excited because it usually brings in new, [interesting features](https://kinsta.com/blog/php-8-1/#new-features-in-php-81) and [performance improvements](https://kinsta.com/blog/php-8-1/#performance-improvements-with-inheritance-cache). That’s exactly what happened with [PHP 8.1](https://kinsta.com/blog/php-8-1/), which was not only released on November 25, 2021, but is now available at Kinsta for all environments.

Before [updating your PHP version](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/php/#updating-php) to PHP 8.1, we’d like to remind you a few things:

- To prevent any data loss, always make sure to [create a backup of your sites](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-backups/#wordpress-backups) before taking any action.
- Test your sites in a [staging environment](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/staging-environment/) first and upgrade your live site only and if you experience no issues.
- WordPress 5.8 (current stable release) doesn’t support PHP 8.1. However, the upcoming WordPress 5.9 version (Jan 25, 2022) will.
- If you do notice issues with your [WordPress version](https://kinsta.com/blog/wordpress-version/), work with a [qualified developer](https://kinsta.com/trusted-tools-for-wordpress/) to [debug your site](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-troubleshooting/wordpress-debugging/#enable-wp-debug) before upgrading to PHP 8.1.
- Not all plugins and themes might be yet compatible with PHP 8.1 and **our support team is unable to assist with code compatibility issues**. Ensure all your plugins and themes support PHP 8.1 before upgrading your PHP version. If you notice a plugin or theme issue on your site, we recommend [downgrading to PHP 8.0](https://kinsta.com/blog/downgrade-wordpress/#6-revert-to-an-older-version-of-php) and reaching out to the developer of the plugin or theme regarding the PHP 8.1 incompatibility.
- PHP 8.1 and PHP 8.0 are not yet compatible with [ionCube Loader](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/mykinsta-tools/#ioncube-loader). If you rely on ionCube Loader for your site, do not upgrade to PHP 8.1 at this time.

And now, let’s see how to enable PHP 8.1 on your sites!

## How to Switch to PHP 8.1

To upgrade to PHP 8.1, log in to [MyKinsta](https://kinsta.com/MyKinsta/) and navigate to your site’s **Tools** tab.

Under the **PHP Engine** option, click **Modify** and **choose** **PHP 8.1** from the dropdown menu:

[![PHP 8.1](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/kinsta-php-8-1.jpg)](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/kinsta-php-8-1.jpg)

Switching to PHP 8.1 in MyKinsta

The upgrade process will take a few minutes and it’ll require your PHP engine to restart. This will make your WordPress backend unavailable for a few seconds only. **Your visitors won’t experience any issues at all**.

Once the upgrade has successfully finished, you’ll receive a notification directly in MyKinsta.

For more information on the exciting upgrades in the latest version of PHP, be sure to check out our [in-depth guide on PHP 8.1](https://kinsta.com/blog/php-8-1/) and [subscribe to our newsletter](https://kinsta.com/wordpress-newsletter/)!
