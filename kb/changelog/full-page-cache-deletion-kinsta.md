# Full Page Cache Deletion From MyKinsta

**Product:** Changelog
**Source:** https://kinsta.com/changelog/full-page-cache-deletion-kinsta/
**Kinsta last updated:** 2026-09-16T09:50:47

---

For Google Cloud customers: A new “Tools” tab has been added to the site management section of the MyKinsta dashboard. The first tool we’ve added is “Cache Management”, you can now delete your site’s full page cache right from your Kinsta dashboard.

Click on any of your sites, switch to the Tools tab and click the “Delete Full Page Cache” button to get it done.
