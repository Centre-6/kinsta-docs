# Account Activity Log Now Available

**Product:** Changelog
**Source:** https://kinsta.com/changelog/account-activity-log-now-available/
**Kinsta last updated:** 2026-09-16T09:58:49

---

We’ve released a great new security and monitoring feature: the Activity Log. The Activity Log allows you to see all the actions that were taken on your account and who these actions were taken by. Log in to your [My Kinsta](https://my.kinsta.com/) dashboard to see the new Activity Log menu item.

[![Kinsta Activity Log](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/activity-log-1024x691.jpg)](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/activity-log.jpg)

Kinsta Activity Log

This feature brings another round of security to our systems by allowing you to easily track all actions. You’ll also be able to see everything we do within your account.
