# Salt Lake City Data Center Is Now Available

**Product:** Changelog
**Source:** https://kinsta.com/changelog/google-cloud-salt-lake-city-data-center/
**Kinsta last updated:** 2026-09-16T09:46:51

---

[Kinsta](https://kinsta.com/why-us/) is powered by Google Cloud Platform’s multi-regional deployment mode that allows you to place your websites in the closest geographical location to your visitors based on the data centers available. This ensures low latency and blazing fast load times.

Starting today, we’re thrilled to announce that a new addition entered the Google Data Center locations list: **Salt Lake City, USA.**

This new data center is **already available** **to all Kinsta users**, on all plans.

Specifically, right from within the [MyKinsta](https://my.kinsta.com/) dashboard, you can now pick “Salt Lake City” as your preferred option when adding a new site.

![Salt Lake City data center in MyKinsta](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Salt-Lake-City-data-center-in-MyKinsta.png)

Salt Lake City data center in MyKinsta

Kinsta now has a total of 30 data center locations you can choose from.

See our knowledge base article on [Google Cloud Platform locations](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/data-center-locations/) for more information and questions about moving an existing site.

Have no idea how to choose the best location for your site? Check out [this quick tutorial](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/data-center-locations/).
