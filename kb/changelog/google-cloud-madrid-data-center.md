# Madrid Data Center Is Now Available

**Product:** Changelog
**Source:** https://kinsta.com/changelog/google-cloud-madrid-data-center/
**Kinsta last updated:** 2026-09-16T09:50:05

---

We’re excited to share that we’ve just added **Madrid**, Spain to the list of [Google Cloud Platform’s data center locations](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/data-center-locations/) available to our customers.

With the addition of Madrid, the total number of data center locations accessible to customers is now 30, which gives site owners even more flexibility in choosing the best geographical location to [increase speed](https://kinsta.com/blog/ttfb/) and [reduce latency](https://kinsta.com/blog/network-latency/).

If you’d like to set Madrid as your data center in MyKinsta, simply follow these steps:

![A screenshot of MyKinsta showing the ](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/madrid-es-gcp-data-center.png)

Host your WordPress site in Madrid, Spain.

1. Navigate to your [MyKinsta dashboard](https://kinsta.com/mykinsta/), click on the **Sites** option on the left sidebar, and then **Add Site** to create a new site.
2. Once there, select Madrid (ES) as your preferred location option from the dropdown menu.

Our new Madrid data center location is **available to all Kinsta customers for free**. If you’re looking for a [WordPress host that offers speed](https://kinsta.com/wordpress-hosting/), flexibility, and a wealth of options, Kinsta is the right choice for you.
