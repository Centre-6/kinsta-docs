Today, we’re happy to introduce you to the latest new features: the new site labeling feature in [MyKinsta](https://kinsta.com/mykinsta/), the new ionCube loader tool, and an addition to our free migrations list.

## External Backups

In addition to snapshot and downloadable backups, MyKinsta now supports external backups as well.

With the new [external backups add-on](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-backups/#external-backups-add-on), you can back up your WordPress site to either [Amazon S3](https://aws.amazon.com/s3/) or [Google Cloud Storage](https://cloud.google.com/storage/).

![Enable external backups in MyKinsta.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/mykinsta-external-backup-addon.jpg)

Enable external backups in MyKinsta.

The external backup feature can be configured to automatically back up your site once per week or per month.

You can also choose to back up your files, database, or both. The external backups add-on is available for $2/month for each site, plus $1/GB for external bandwidth.

Here’s a sample price calculation for three sites using external backups.

- Site A uses 5 GB of external bandwidth: $2 + $5
- Site B uses 8 GB of external bandwidth: $2 + $8
- Site C uses 20 GB of external bandwidth: $2 + $20

For the three sites above, the total cost per month for external backups is $39.

For more information on the external backup add-on and how to integrate it with Amazon S3 and Google Cloud Storage, refer to [this article](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-backups/#external-backups-add-on).

## Site Labels in MyKinsta

If you manage a large number of WordPress sites on Kinsta, keeping your list of sites organized is important. To help you organize your sites efficiently, we’ve updated MyKinsta with a site labeling feature.

Similar to [WordPress’ categories and tags](https://kinsta.com/blog/what-is-taxonomy/), this new tool allows you to assign custom labels to your Kinsta sites for easy filtering and categorization.

![Add labels to your WordPress sites.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/mykinsta-label-sites.jpg)

Add labels to your WordPress sites.

To learn how to label and organize your sites, check out our [knowledgebase article](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/site-management/#label-sites-in-mykinsta).

## Enable ionCube Loader in MyKinsta

If your WordPress makes use of PHP scripts protected with [ionCube’s encoder](https://kinsta.com/blog/ioncube-loader/), you’ll need to enable the ionCube Loader extension to decode and run the scripts. In the past, you had to [reach out to our support team](https://kinsta.com/docs/support/contact-support/) and submit a request for that.

Starting today, you won’t need to do that at all because we’ve made it easy for you to enable ionCube directly from within MyKinsta, where you now find the new ionCube Loader tool.

Simply navigate to your site’s **Tools** page and click the “Enable” button under ionCube Loader.

![Enable ionCube Loader in MyKinsta.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/mykinsta-enable-ioncube-loader-2.jpg)

Enable ionCube Loader in MyKinsta.

Recently, we added [WPX Hosting](https://kinsta.com/wpx-hosting-alternative/) to the list. No matter how many sites you host at WPX Hosting, we’ll be able to move them all to Kinsta free of charge! Check out our [free migrations page](https://kinsta.com/wordpress-hosting/migration/) for more information on how to move your site from WPX Hosting to Kinsta.
