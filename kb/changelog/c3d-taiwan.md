# Kinsta’s platform is supercharged in Taiwan with Google’s C3D VMs

**Product:** Changelog
**Source:** https://kinsta.com/changelog/c3d-taiwan/
**Kinsta last updated:** 2026-09-16T09:57:24

---

Kinsta’s [Managed WordPress Hosting](https://kinsta.com/wordpress-hosting/) platform is moving to Google Cloud’s fastest virtual machines in its Taiwan data center.

Also known as asia-east1, the Taiwan data center is now the third in the region that Kinsta has dedicated to Google’s [C3D VMs](https://kinsta.com/blog/wordpress-performance-benchmarks/), following their adoption in Jurong West (asia-southeast1) in Singapore and Mumbai (asia-south1) in India.

Outside of Asia, Kinsta customers currently have access to the new VMs in eight other data centers:

- Sydney, Australia (australia-southeast1)
- St. Ghislain, Belgium (europe-west1)
- Frankfurt, Germany (europe-west3)
- Eemshaven, Netherlands (europe-west4)
- Council Bluffs, Iowa, USA (us-central1)
- Moncks Corner, South Carolina, USA (us-east1)
- Ashburn, Virginia, USA (us-east4)
- Las Vegas, Nevada, USA (us-west4)

We’re migrating existing customer websites in Taiwan to the new technology for free. Meanwhile, if you are creating a new website, C3D-powered data centers like Tawain and the others above are highlighted in [MyKinsta](https://kinsta.com/mykinsta/) with the **Boosted** indicator:

![A screenshot showing the data-center selection menu in MyKinsta with some locations — including Taiwan — flagged as Boosted.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/mykinsta-taiwan-c3d-boosted.png)

Data centers featuring C3D virtual machines are flagged as **Boosted** in MyKinsta.

At Kinsta, we aim to provide customers with the fastest and most reliable hosting anywhere. We’ve built our service on the most advanced virtual machines Google Cloud has available in each data center. In many locations, the fastest offering is still Google’s [compute-optimized C2 VMs](https://kinsta.com/changelog/google-cloud-platform-c2-vms/). But we’ll be moving to the more powerful C3D technology wherever it becomes available at scale across the 30 data centers used by our customers.

Kinsta’s [testing of C3D-based VMs](https://kinsta.com/blog/wordpress-performance-benchmarks/) found 20% to 50% response time improvements over those already-fast C2 machines.

[What makes the C3D machines so fast](https://kinsta.com/blog/wordpress-performance-benchmarks/) is technology that includes AMD’s EPYC™ processor and an infrastructure processing unit (or IPU) that offloads network and storage I/O from the CPU.

C2 VMs offer up to 60 virtual CPUs and 240 gigabytes of RAM. C3Ds serve up six times those resources — as many as 360 virtual CPUs and 2.88 terabytes of DDR5 memory.
