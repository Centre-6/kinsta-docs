![An illustration representing Kinsta's Managed WordPress Hosting pricing page.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/wordpress-hosting-plan-names-1024x536.png)

Kinsta’s [Managed WordPress Hosting](https://kinsta.com/wordpress-hosting/) plans have new names to better reflect the resources behind each.

Gone are vague labels like “Business 1 Single” and “Enterprise 3.” Instead, all single-site plans now have names that indicate the monthly visitor limit included in the price (like “Single 35k”), while the names of multiple-site plans reveal the number of WordPress installs each provides (like “WP 120”).

What’s more, we’ve increased the visitor limits and [Kinsta CDN](https://kinsta.com/blog/cloudflare-integration/) resources across all plans and added a new single-site plan with a 65,000 visitor threshold and 250 GB of CDN bandwidth.

Here’s a look at the new plan names and resources compared with the previous offerings:
