# Our BWP 3 Plan Now Has More Installs

**Product:** Changelog
**Source:** https://kinsta.com/changelog/business-1-plan-more-installs/
**Kinsta last updated:** 2026-09-16T09:57:43

---

We’ve heard your feedback and are happy to announce that we just added two more installs to our [WP 3 plan](https://kinsta.com/pricing/?plan=visits-business1) at no extra cost. 🎉

Previously our WP 3 plan had three installs. A lot of you manage multiple websites or work with clients, and this simply wasn’t enough. Therefore, we’ve added two more, bringing it to**five WordPress installs on our WP 3 plan and making it our WP 5 plan now**.

This also makes it much more cost effective when you’re calculating out your monthly hosting spend per WordPress site.

Remember, if you’re running a multisite setup, this is still considered one install due to the fact that they share a database.

We hope this increase in installs will help you bring even more WordPress sites over to Kinsta! As always, keep the feedback coming, we greatly appreciate it.
