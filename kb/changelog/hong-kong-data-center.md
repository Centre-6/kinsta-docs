# 18th Data Center in Hong Kong Is Now Available

**Product:** Changelog
**Source:** https://kinsta.com/changelog/hong-kong-data-center/
**Kinsta last updated:** 2026-09-16T09:45:22

---

Further increasing Kinsta’s reach in Asia, we are excited to announce that the data center in **Hong Kong**(asia-east2) is now available for hosting your WordPress sites. 🇭🇰

This will be the fifth data we’ve launched in 2018. Throughout the past year we’ve added the following locations:

- Los Angeles – launched July 2018
- Finland – launched June 2018
- Montreal – launched January 2018
- Netherlands – launched January 2018

Kinsta now has 18 [Google Cloud data centers](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/data-center-locations/) you can choose from (this is the fifth in Asia Pacific), allowing you to place your website in a geographical location closest to your visitors. This ensures better data privacy, low latency, and blazing fast load times.

According to [Internet World Stats](https://www.internetworldstats.com/asia.htm#hk), Hong Kong alone has over 6.4 million internet users. It’s known for being an international commercial hub and has one of the world’s most open and dynamic economies.

> A solid cloud infrastructure is the foundation for building a smart city and helping businesses succeed in the digital economy. We are glad that Google is launching the Hong Kong cloud region, a recognition of Hong Kong’s edge and strengths as a data hub. This means businesses in Hong Kong, whether big or small, can leverage the latest, well-established technology infrastructure to expand and succeed in the region and globally. — Nicholas W Yang, Secretary for Innovation & Technology, HKSAR Government ([Source](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/gcp/coming-in-2018-gcps-hong-kong-region?m=1))

Google has [stated](https://cloud.google.com/about/locations/hongkong/) that this new region will significantly **improve latency for end users in Hong Kong** and surrounding regions. Previously the closest available data center was in Taiwan. But don’t take our word for it, you can use [GCP Ping](http://www.gcping.com/) to see for yourself how fast the Hong Kong region is from your location.

And don’t forget, the [Kinsta CDN](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-cdn/kinsta-cdn/#kinstas-cdn) also has a location in Hong Kong, as well as one in Taiwan.

### Launch a Site in Hong Kong Today

This new location is now available from within the [MyKinsta](https://my.kinsta.com/) dashboard when you go to add a site: Hong Kong (HK).
