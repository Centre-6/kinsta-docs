# More Disk Space (SSD Storage) on All Plans

**Product:** Changelog
**Source:** https://kinsta.com/changelog/more-disk-space/
**Kinsta last updated:** 2026-09-16T09:40:22

---

We’ve done it again! [Back in 2018](https://kinsta.com/changelog/ssh-disk-space-free-migration/), we added more disk space to our Single 35k, WP 2, and Single 125k plans. While many were happy about this, some still weren’t satisfied with the disk space amounts across the board. If we don’t get something right the first time around, you can guarantee we’ll revisit it.

You’ll be happy to hear that **we’ve added more disk space on all of our standard plans** (from Single 35k through WP 60 and beyond). On some of the plans, in fact, we’ve doubled the amount! All at no extra cost. 🎉

These changes should already be reflected in your MyKinsta dashboard. For those of you on our standard [legacy plans](https://kinsta.com/docs/billing/wordpress-hosting-plans/#legacy-plan-details), the same increases have been applied.

Need something bigger? We have custom plans available too.

We hope you like this change. We have another exciting feature update coming in the next couple days. Stay tuned!
