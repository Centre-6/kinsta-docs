# Boosting Monthly Visits for Starter and Pro Plans

**Product:** Changelog
**Source:** https://kinsta.com/changelog/boosting-visits-starter-pro-plans/
**Kinsta last updated:** 2026-09-16T09:57:05

---

Today, we have some exciting news to share for our customers on [Starter and Pro plans](https://kinsta.com/pricing/). At Kinsta, we’re constantly looking for ways to improve our WordPress hosting experience with the goal of adding more value for our users. The most recent example of this is our [infrastructure migration to Google Cloud Platform’s C2 virtual machines](https://kinsta.com/blog/wordpress-performance/), which gave WordPress sites hosted on Kinsta up to a **200% performance boost at no additional cost** since these are the [fastest Google Cloud servers](https://kinsta.com/wordpress-hosting/) available.

In the spirit of providing more value to our customers, we are happy to announce that Starter and Pro plans will be receiving a **25% increase** in [monthly visits](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-analytics-mykinsta/counting-visits/) at no additional cost!

- **Single 35k plans now support 25,000 visits per month** (20,000 previously).
- **WP 2 plans now support 50,000 visits per month** (40,000 previously).

To make things easy, the upgraded monthly visit count will be applied to all Starter and Pro plans automatically. **You won’t have to opt-in or do anything on your end.** Lastly, the visit increase applies to all variations of our Starter and Pro plans – monthly, annual, and [nonprofit](https://kinsta.com/wordpress-hosting/nonprofits/).

At Kinsta, we’re passionate about building a WordPress hosting platform that our customers love, and we’re always open to hearing your feedback. To stay up to date with future updates, don’t forget to [subscribe to our newsletter](https://kinsta.com/wordpress-newsletter/)!
