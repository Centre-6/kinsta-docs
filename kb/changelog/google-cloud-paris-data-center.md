# Paris Data Center Is Now Available

**Product:** Changelog
**Source:** https://kinsta.com/changelog/google-cloud-paris-data-center/
**Kinsta last updated:** 2026-09-16T09:47:54

---

The list of [Google Cloud Platform’s data center locations](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/data-center-locations/) available to our customers has expanded again! Our newest addition is **Paris**, making it the first location available in France.

Already **accessible on all plans for free**, this Paris location brings our total number of data centers up to 30. With so many to choose from, customers can ensure their sites [load even quicker](https://kinsta.com/blog/ttfb/) for their visitors by [preventing unnecessary latency](https://kinsta.com/blog/network-latency/).

Here’s how to set Paris as your data center in MyKinsta:

![A screenshot of MyKinsta showing the "Add Site" location drop-down set to "Paris (FR)".](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/milan-it-gcp-data-center-1.png)

Host your WordPress site in Paris, France.

1. Navigate to your [MyKinsta dashboard](https://kinsta.com/mykinsta/), click on the **Sites** option on the left sidebar, and then **Add Site** to create a new site.
2. Once there, select Paris (FR) as your preferred location option from the dropdown menu.

Kinsta is powered by both [Google Cloud Platform’s C2 VMs](https://kinsta.com/wordpress-hosting/) and a [low-latency Premium Tier network](https://kinsta.com/blog/cloud-platform-for-developers/). By choosing Kinsta as your [managed WordPress host](https://kinsta.com/wordpress-hosting/), you’re gifting your audience a faster, more enjoyable visit to your site.
