Releasing features we hope you never have to use isn’t our usual style, but we admit that the ability to rapidly take a site offline and make it inaccessible to visitors and administrators is one.

On the bright side, the new **Suspend Environment** feature is as easy — and quick — to undo as it is to enable in the first place.

If you’re asking, “What’s the point?” you might be lucky enough to have never had a billing dispute with your own customers. And, while it’s not the only possible reason to suspend site access, requests for this feature have most often come from agencies and resellers who needed a way to get tough with customers without completely dismantling a website.

## Suspending a WordPress environment

Visitors can’t view a suspended environment on the public-facing side, and site operators can’t log in to its WordPress admin dashboard. Users with only Site Administrator or Site Developer roles in MyKinsta will not have access to dashboard tools that help manage the environment or access its resources.

To enable a suspension, navigate to **Sites** > ***sitename*** to reach the **Site Information** page. Scroll down to reach the red **Suspend Environment** button:

![A screenshot showing the Suspend Environment button in MyKinsta.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/mykinsta-site-suspend-environment.png)

Start a WordPress environment suspension in MyKinsta.

After clicking the button, you will be asked to confirm the environment suspension and optionally provide a reason for the suspension that will be visible to anyone with access to MyKinsta:

![A screenshot of the environment suspension confirmation dialog in MyKinsta.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/mykinsta-site-suspend-environment-confirmation.png)

Adding a notice message and confirming site suspension.

After suspension is enabled, visitors to the environment’s **Site Information** page in MyKinsta will see a banner like the one below:

![A screenshot of the environment suspension notice on the Site Information page in MyKinsta.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/mykinsta-site-suspend-environment-notice.png)

The environment suspension notice on the Site Information page.

MyKinsta users with access to reverse the suspension (Company Owners and Company Administrators) will see the **Activate Environment** button. Others will see only the notice text shown during suspension confirmation.

Meanwhile, anyone attempting to access the WordPress site will see a connection timeout message in their browser.

It’s as easy as that (and we hope you never have to use it!)
