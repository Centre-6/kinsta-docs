# Tel Aviv Data Center Is Now Available

**Product:** Changelog
**Source:** https://kinsta.com/changelog/google-cloud-tel-aviv-data-center/
**Kinsta last updated:** 2026-09-16T09:45:02

---

Our list of accessible [Google Cloud Platform data centers](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/data-center-locations/) has grown yet again! Kinsta customers can now choose **Tel Aviv**, Israel, from our list of 30 globally positioned data centers.

If your business relies on traffic from the area in or around Tel Aviv, choosing it as your website’s data center location will immediately [speed up load times](https://kinsta.com/blog/ttfb/) for your user base by [reducing latency](https://kinsta.com/blog/network-latency/) across your site.

It is, as the saying goes, all about location, location, location!

Tel Aviv is already **available on all Kinsta plans for free**. To set it as your data center, just follow these steps in MyKinsta:

1. Navigate to your [MyKinsta dashboard](https://kinsta.com/mykinsta/) and click on the **WordPress** option in the left sidebar.
2. Click the **Add site** button at the top.
3. Select **Tel Aviv (IS)** as your preferred location option from the **Data center location** dropdown menu.

![A screenshot of MyKinsta showing the &quot;Add Site&quot; location drop-down set to "Tel Aviv (IS)".](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/mykinsta-tel-aviv-israel-gcp-data-center.png)

Host your WordPress site in Tel Aviv, Israel.

Every site hosted at Kinsta is powered by Google Cloud’s blazing-fast [C2 VMs](https://kinsta.com/wordpress-hosting/) and [low-latency Premium Tier network](https://kinsta.com/blog/cloud-platform-for-developers/). Kinsta’s [WordPress hosting plans](https://kinsta.com/pricing/) are designed to get you to the finish line faster than your competitors — because we know that speed is the name of the game.
