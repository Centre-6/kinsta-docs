# Milan Data Center Is Now Available

**Product:** Changelog
**Source:** https://kinsta.com/changelog/google-cloud-milan-data-center/
**Kinsta last updated:** 2026-09-16T09:48:15

---

We’re thrilled to announce that **Milan**, Italy, has joined [Google Cloud Platform’s data center locations](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/data-center-locations/), and it’s already **available for free on all Kinsta plans**.

This gives Kinsta customers even more flexibility in choosing how their users are served. By selecting a data center close to your user base, you’ll immediately [speed up your site](https://kinsta.com/cheatsheets/site-speed-cheat-sheet/) and [reduce latency](https://kinsta.com/blog/network-latency/) for your visitors.

To set Milan as your data center in MyKinsta, just follow these steps:

![A screenshot of MyKinsta showing the ](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/milan-it-gcp-data-center.png)

Host your WordPress site in Milan, Italy.

1. Navigate to your [MyKinsta dashboard](https://kinsta.com/mykinsta/), click on the **Sites** option on the left sidebar, and then **Add Site** to create a new site.
2. Once there, select Milan (IT) as your preferred location option from the dropdown menu.

With 30 total data center locations to choose from, Kinsta is the perfect host for any [speed-seeking WordPress site](https://kinsta.com/blog/cloudflare-integration/) owner.
