# Multiple Payment Methods Now Supported

**Product:** Changelog
**Source:** https://kinsta.com/changelog/multiple-payment-methods-now-supported/
**Kinsta last updated:** 2026-09-16T09:39:36

---

Credit cards can be difficult to keep track of. They can get lost or expire without us noticing. To make sure billing goes smoothly we’ve built in the option for you to provide multiple credit/debit cards.

We’ll always charge your primary card when possible and fall back on backups if a problem is detected. If you need help setting up multiple credit/debit cards take a look at our [knowledgebase article](https://kinsta.com/docs/billing/payments/#add-multiple-credit-debit-cards).
