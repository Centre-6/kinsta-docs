Kinsta’s [Managed WordPress Hosting](https://kinsta.com/wordpress-hosting/) customers can now increase the number of sites they manage without making a leap to a higher-tier plan.

Customers who need just a little more elbow room in their current plan might find it cost-effective to use the new **WordPress sites add-on** to increase their plan’s site quota for just $30 USD per site a month. For many, that will be less than the cost of moving all the way to the next-higher hosting plan.

## How to use the WordPress sites add-on

This add-on works at the Company level within the [MyKinsta dashboard](https://kinsta.com/mykinsta/). Start by navigating to **username** > **Company settings** > **My Plan**. On the **My Plan** page, click the **Enable** button on the **WordPress sites** card:

![Screenshot of the My Plan page in MyKinsta showing the WordPress sites add-on option.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/mykinsta-add-ons-wordpress-sites.png)

Adding a WordPress site to a hosting plan.

Next, you can specify the number of sites you want to add to your current plan:

![Screenshot of the WordPress sites add-on dialog within MyKinsta.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/mykinsta-add-ons-wordpess-sites-dialog.png)

Choosing the number of sites to add to a hosting plan.

## When to use the WordPress sites add-on

We recommend reviewing our [Managed WordPress Hosting Pricing](https://kinsta.com/pricing/) page to determine whether adding sites to your current plan is more cost-effective than upgrading the plan itself.

It’s important to note that adding a site to your current plan does not increase plan-wide resources such as limits on visits, CDN usage, and disk space (although you can [increase the disk space](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-add-ons/#disk-space-add-on) available to your plan separately).

The **WordPress analytics** page within **Company settings** can help you determine if your current plan usage leaves room for more sites:

![Screenshot of the Analytics page within MyKinsta showing plan-wide resource use.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/mykinsta-add-ons-wordpess-sites-analytics.png)

A summary of plan-wide resource usage within WordPress analytics.

The analytics report above shows that this company has maxed out its WordPress site quota but probably has plenty of resources available to accommodate more using the site add-on.

## Adding up Kinsta’s WordPress add-ons

In addition to the disk space add-on that works plan-wide, Kinsta offers several [site-specific add-ons](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-add-ons/) so that you can equip your WordPress environments with exactly the resources you need. Those add-ons support:

- Premium staging environments
- PHP performance
- Reverse proxies
- Redis caching
- Hourly and six-hour backups
- External backups
- Disk space
- Dedicated servers
- Add WordPress sites

If you are not already a Kinsta customer and are not sure which WordPress hosting plan might be right for you, just [contact our sales team](https://kinsta.com/contact-us/).
