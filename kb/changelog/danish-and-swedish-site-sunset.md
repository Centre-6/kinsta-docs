![](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/dk-se-site-sunset-1024x536.png)

We have decided to sunset the Danish and Swedish versions of our website and platform as part of our ongoing efforts to streamline and improve the overall Kinsta product and content experience.

## What’s changing?

As part of this change, the Danish and Swedish versions of our website will be discontinued and will no longer be available. There’s no need to update bookmarks or links; everything will continue to work seamlessly.

We’ll also be removing Danish and Swedish as language options in [MyKinsta](https://my.kinsta.com/login) and [DevKinsta](https://kinsta.com/devkinsta/). If you’re currently using one of these languages, your dashboard will automatically switch to English once the change takes effect.

Finally, general support for DK and SE won’t be available after the change

## What you can expect

All features and functionality will remain exactly the same, and you’ll continue to have full access to the platform. You can expect a smooth transition with no disruption to your experience.

All features and functionality will remain fully available in English, so you’ll still be able to use the platform exactly as before.

Any existing links or bookmarks will continue to work, as pages will automatically redirect to their English equivalents. You’ll also continue to have access to our global support team, who are available to assist you whenever you need help.

## Why we’re making this change

This decision allows us to focus our resources on improving performance, content quality, and product development across the languages and markets where we can deliver the most impact. Our goal is to ensure a more consistent and high-quality experience for all users.

## What you should do

No action is required on your end. You can continue using Kinsta in English or any of the other supported languages, and our support team will remain available to assist you as always.

We appreciate your understanding as we continue to improve Kinsta and deliver the best possible experience. If you have any questions, our support team is here to help.
