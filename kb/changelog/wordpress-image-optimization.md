Attention, WordPress users! We’re excited to announce the release of our new free Image Optimization feature for all customers on Kinsta’s Managed WordPress Hosting.

Images can account for a massive amount of your overall storage and bandwidth. If not [managed properly](https://kinsta.com/blog/optimize-images-for-web/), these images can [slow down your site](https://kinsta.com/blog/wordpress-slow/) by increasing your load times, leading to [decreased traffic](https://kinsta.com/blog/website-traffic-analysis/) and unhappy users.

Now, [Kinsta’s Image Optimization](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-cdn/kinsta-cdn/#image-optimization-1) can help you turn that around in just a few clicks.

## Introducing Image Optimization for WordPress

This new feature automatically creates optimized [WebP copies](https://kinsta.com/blog/webp/#what-is-webp) of your original images (which are stored at no additional cost to you in terms of disk space). Then, taking advantage of [Kinsta’s CDN](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-cdn/kinsta-cdn/#kinstas-cdn), it serves up the smaller of the two image files to your end users.

By always ensuring the smallest possible file is retrieved and delivered, Image Optimization immediately [boosts loading times](https://kinsta.com/blog/ttfb/), thereby improving user experience and [cultivating customer loyalty](https://kinsta.com/blog/trust-badges/). Image optimization is also an excellent way to improve your SEO results.

## Implementing Image Optimization

Using Image Optimization is simple: Just upload your images to your [WordPress media library](https://kinsta.com/blog/wordpress-media-library/), follow the prompts through your [MyKinsta dashboard](https://kinsta.com/mykinsta/) to enable optimization, then let the system take care of the rest.

![A screenshot of the CDN settings page in MyKinsta with the Image Optimization option highlighted.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/mykinsta-enable-image-optimization-changelog.png)

Enabling image optimization in MyKinsta.

Here are the steps to follow:

1. Upload your images to your Kinsta-hosted WordPress media library.
2. Sign in to your MyKinsta dashboard.
3. Navigate to **WordPress Sites > *sitename* > **Caching**.**
4. Select the **CDN** tab at the top of the page.
5. Click the **Settings** button in the **Image optimization** section.
6. Choose between lossless and lossy compression, then click **Save Settings**.

![Choose from Lossy or Lossless compression, then click the ](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/mykinsta-image-optimization-options-changelog.png)

Choose from lossless or lossy compression.

### Lossless vs lossy compression

You can choose between [lossless and lossy compression](https://kinsta.com/blog/lossy-vs-lossless/) for your files. Although both methods will compress your image files and remove their metadata, there’s a big difference between them:

- **Lossless Image Optimization:** Lossless image compression has zero impact on the quality of your images. You should pick this option if image quality is more important to you than speed.
- **Lossy Image Optimization:** [Lossy image compression](https://kinsta.com/blog/lossy-compression/) might reduce the quality of your images. Pick this option to maximize and prioritize speed over image quality.

Not only will this improve the speed of your site, but it will help your site [rank higher in search engine results](https://kinsta.com/blog/wordpress-seo/), leading to [more traffic](https://kinsta.com/blog/conversion-rate-optimization-tips/) and new potential customers, too.
