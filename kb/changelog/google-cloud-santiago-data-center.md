# Santiago Data Center Is Now Available

**Product:** Changelog
**Source:** https://kinsta.com/changelog/google-cloud-santiago-data-center/
**Kinsta last updated:** 2026-09-16T09:46:05

---

We’re excited to announce that the [Google Cloud Platform’s data center locations](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/data-center-locations/) keep growing. The latest data center location available is **Santiago**, Chile, which becomes the second location available in South America besides São Paulo, Brazil.

The new data center location, which is already **available for free on all plans**, gives more options to Kinsta customers for setting their newly created websites and online stores closer to their visitors and users, hence [reducing latency](https://kinsta.com/blog/network-latency/) and [improving load times](https://kinsta.com/blog/ttfb/).

Here’s how to set Santiago as your data center in MyKinsta:

[![Santiago Data Center Is Now Available](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/santiago-chile-gcp-data-center-1.jpg)](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/santiago-chile-gcp-data-center-1.jpg)

Host your WordPress site in Santiago, Chile.

1. Navigate to your [MyKinsta dashboard](https://kinsta.com/mykinsta/), click on the **Sites** option on the left sidebar, and then **Add Site** to create a new site.
2. Once there, select Santiago (CL) as your preferred location option from the dropdown menu.

Powered by [Google Cloud Platform’s C2 VMs](https://kinsta.com/wordpress-hosting/), [low-latency Premium Tier network](https://kinsta.com/blog/cloud-platform-for-developers/), and 30 data center locations to choose from, Kinsta is the right host for WordPress sites.
