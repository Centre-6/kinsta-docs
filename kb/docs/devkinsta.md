# - DevKinsta

**Product:** DevKinsta (local dev tool, not hosting)
**Source:** https://kinsta.com/docs/devkinsta/
**Kinsta last updated:** January 9, 2025

---

DevKinsta is a local development suite for WordPress sites. You can [create WordPress sites](https://kinsta.com/docs/devkinsta/devkinsta-getting-started/creating-a-site/) with some basic details, and the required components and database will be installed in the background. You will only need your favorite text editor to get started.

![DevKinsta site information. ](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/devkinsta-dashboard.png)

DevKinsta site information.

The WordPress environment comes with an [Email Inbox](https://kinsta.com/docs/devkinsta/devkinsta-site-management/email-inbox/), [Database Manager](https://kinsta.com/docs/devkinsta/devkinsta-database/database-manager/), and [Logging](https://kinsta.com/docs/devkinsta/devkinsta-troubleshooting/logging/) for easier troubleshooting.

DevKinsta is fully [compatible](https://kinsta.com/docs/devkinsta/devkinsta-integration/) with your existing Kinsta Hosting Plan, but a Kinsta Hosting Plan or account isn’t required to use DevKinsta on your local machine.

If you need further assistance, don’t hesitate to join our [Community Forum](https://community.kinsta.com/c/devkinsta/14).

Get started by downloading and installing DevKinsta for your operating system:

- [Windows](https://kinsta.com/docs/devkinsta/devkinsta-getting-started/installation/#windows)
- [Mac](https://kinsta.com/docs/devkinsta/devkinsta-getting-started/installation/#mac)
- [Linux](https://kinsta.com/docs/devkinsta/devkinsta-getting-started/installation/#linux)
