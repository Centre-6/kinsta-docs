# Service Information

**Product:** Service Information
**Source:** https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/
**Kinsta last updated:** 2026-02-19T09:14:10

---

The service information area of our documentation includes information about Kinsta’s services and policies, including the following:

- [System Status](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/status-page): View the operational status for all data centers, our website, and MyKinsta.
- [Infrastructure Upgrades](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/infrastructure-upgrades/): Infrastructure upgrades enable us to leverage the latest technologies, enhance security, optimize system performance, and ensure your sites continue to run on the most advanced and reliable hosting environment available. Find out how we notify you of upgrades and our most-asked questions about them.
- [Data Center Locations](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/data-center-locations/): See which data centers are available for each of our hosting services.
- [File and Database Storage](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/files-and-database-storage): Find out what data related to your website’s visitors, files, and the database may be securely transmitted elsewhere.
- [Guaranteed Uptime](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/guaranteed-uptime/): View our uptime guarantee backed by our Service Level Agreement (SLA).
- [Money-Back Guarantee](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/money-back-guarantee/): View our Money-Back Guarantee policy for WordPress Hosting.
- [Malware Removal](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/malware-removal): Find out how Kinsta can help you identify malware and repair your WordPress site.
- [Sevalla](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/sevalla-overview/): Learn more about our purpose-built platform for Application, Database, and Static Site Hosting.
- [Research Program](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/kinsta-research-program/): Find out how to join Kinsta’s Research Program to share your thoughts on hosting services, tell us about how you work, or test and give feedback on new features.
- [Affiliate Program](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/affiliate-program): Find out how to join Kinsta’s Affiliate Program, where you’ll receive **10% monthly commission** for the lifetime of a referred customer in addition to **up to $500** one-time payment for WordPress referrals.
- [Referral Program](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/referrals-faq/): Find out how to earn credits by referring others to Kinsta with a special referral link through Kinsta’s Referral Program.
- [Allowed Content](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/prohibited-content/): Find out what content types are prohibited from Kinsta’s hosting services.
- [Disclose a Vulnerability](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/disclose-security-vulnerability/): How to disclose a security vulnerability and alert our team to the issue privately so we can respond to the issue and ensure our platform is secure and customer data protected.
- [Russian Accounts & Domains](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/new-accounts-from-russia): This article explains why we cannot accept new clients from Russia at this time.
- [SOC 2](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/soc-2/): Find out more about SOC 2 and where to get a copy of Kinsta’s SOC 2 report.
- [Security Roles and Responsibilities](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/security-roles-responsibilities): Explains the security responsibilities for the various aspects of Kinsta.

If you encounter anything not included in these documents, please let us know using the feedback options below.
