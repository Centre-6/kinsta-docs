## Managed WordPress Hosting services

Our [Managed WordPress Hosting](https://kinsta.com/wordpress-hosting/) includes a global network of premium data centers designed to deliver exceptional speed, stability, and scalability. It also includes automatic updates of OS-level security patches, daily backups (both files and databases with a [minimum 14-day retention](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-backups/#backup-retention)), and our [Security Pledge](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/malware-removal/).

## Security

We keep your site safe from the infrastructure side with free hack fixes through our [Security Pledge](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/malware-removal/) and cloud-based 1st layer defense through our [Cloudflare integration](https://kinsta.com/blog/cloudflare-integration/) on all Managed WordPress sites hosted at Kinsta. Our Cloudflare integration helps to protect against code and [SQL injection](https://kinsta.com/blog/sql-injection/), Layer 7 [DDoS attacks](https://kinsta.com/blog/what-is-a-ddos-attack/), and generic malware/vulnerability scanners.

You will still be responsible for ensuring the security of your site’s application stack, including updating plugins and themes.

## Uptime

In addition, [we’re responsible for uptime](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/guaranteed-uptime/), including infrastructure and OS-level configurations. We monitor your site’s uptime and respond to any issues that cause the site not to load. You can also enable [site monitoring notifications](https://kinsta.com/docs/user-settings/notifications/) to receive emails for critical errors that can affect your site’s uptime.

## Automatic MySQL database optimizations

Though it can be an important factor in ensuring fast performance and long-term health of a WordPress site, optimizing the database often gets neglected.

To combat database performance woes, we offer a service that **automatically fine-tunes your MySQL database settings** based on your WordPress site’s needs**.** This optimization runs every minute and ensures better [MariaDB](https://kinsta.com/blog/mariadb-vs-mysql/) performance.

### Important

While we’re proud of this awesome new feature, it doesn’t mean you can ignore best practices though, such as:

- [Uninstalling plugins the right way](https://kinsta.com/blog/uninstall-wordpress-plugin/) to remove left-behind database tables.
- Regularly checking your [wp_options table for autoloaded data](https://kinsta.com/blog/wp-options-autoloaded-data/) or using a plugin like WP Rocket to clean up left-behind revisions, transients, etc.

## Pricing

With Kinsta’s WordPress Hosting, you choose the [plan](https://kinsta.com/docs/billing/wordpress-hosting-plans/#add-a-new-plan) that best suits your site, and you can easily upgrade or switch your plan at any time if required. Our WordPress services use a pay-forward billing method where you pay for your usage in advance of your bill.

If you reach your plan’s resource limits, we keep your sites running but charge a fee for any [overages](https://kinsta.com/docs/billing/wordpress-hosting-plans/overages/). Overage notifications are enabled in MyKinsta by default, so you’ll be notified in advance about any overages.

Please read our [Managed WordPress scope of support](https://kinsta.com/docs/support/scope-of-support/managed-wordpress-scope-of-support/) for more details.
