The DevKinsta troubleshooting documentation helps you to diagnose and resolve issues within DevKinsta and your WordPress sites. It includes:

- [Docker Issues](https://kinsta.com/docs/devkinsta/devkinsta-troubleshooting/devkinsta-docker-issues/): Troubleshooting tips for issues with Docker when installing DevKinsta.
- [Logs and Debugging](https://kinsta.com/docs/devkinsta/devkinsta-troubleshooting/logging): Find out how to enable WordPress debugging, how to access your log files, and what they include.
- [Xdebug support](https://kinsta.com/docs/devkinsta/devkinsta-troubleshooting/xdebug-support): Explains how to install Xdebug for debugging PHP.
- [Error Codes](https://kinsta.com/docs/devkinsta/devkinsta-troubleshooting/error-codes): This includes errors you may encounter in DevKinsta and how to resolve them.
- [Windows Issues](https://kinsta.com/docs/devkinsta/devkinsta-troubleshooting/switch-docker-wsl-to-hyper-v): Explains how to resolve any slowness encountered in DevKinsta on the Windows operating system.
