## How running a WordPress site works

Most sites go through the same stages: launch, a settling-in period, and then a long stretch of routine upkeep, with occasional bigger changes as the site grows. Kinsta has tools for each stage.

- **Launch:** Building or migrating your site and pointing your domain to it. Free migrations, a temporary URL, Site Preview, and free SSL help here. See the [Go Live Checklist](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-getting-started/go-live-checklist/).
- **Settle in:** Tying up loose ends from launch and your old host, with monitoring notifications, PHP settings, and plugin management. See [After Go-Live: First Steps](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/using-kinsta/kinsta-first-steps/).
- **Tune:** Making the site fast and secure with the CDN, edge caching, APM, bot protection, and vulnerability protection. See [Speed Up Your Site](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/using-kinsta/kinsta-speed-up-your-site/) and [Protect Your Site](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/using-kinsta/kinsta-protect-your-site/).
- **Day-to-day:** Updates, backups, and regular checks, supported by Automatic Updates, daily backups, uptime monitoring, and vulnerability scans. See [Your Maintenance Routine](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/using-kinsta/kinsta-your-maintenance-routine/).
- **Change:** Redesigns, new plugins, and WordPress upgrades, made safely with staging, selective push, and manual backups. See [Make Changes Safely](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/using-kinsta/kinsta-make-changes-safely/).
- **Grow:** More traffic, more people, and more sites, with Analytics, add-ons, user roles, bulk actions, and developer tools. See [Understand Your Traffic and Usage](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/using-kinsta/kinsta-understand-your-traffic-and-usage/), [Work as a Team](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/using-kinsta/kinsta-work-as-a-team/), and [Developer Workflows](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/using-kinsta/kinsta-developer-workflows/).

## Not sure where to begin?

If you’re new to Kinsta, [After Go-Live: First Steps](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/using-kinsta/kinsta-first-steps/), [Speed Up Your Site](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/using-kinsta/kinsta-speed-up-your-site/), [Protect Your Site](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/using-kinsta/kinsta-protect-your-site/), and [Make Changes Safely](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/using-kinsta/kinsta-make-changes-safely/) apply to every site, whatever its size. [Your Maintenance Routine](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/using-kinsta/kinsta-your-maintenance-routine/) covers what to check from then on. The other pages become useful as your site, team, or workflow grows.

If your site isn’t live yet, see the [Go Live Checklist](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-getting-started/go-live-checklist/). Our Support team is available 24/7 if you get stuck.
