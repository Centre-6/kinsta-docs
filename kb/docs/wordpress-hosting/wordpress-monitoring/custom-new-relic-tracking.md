[New Relic](https://newrelic.com/) is a monitoring tool that gives you detailed performance breakdowns on a granular level.

To monitor your website with New Relic, you’ll need to sign up for your own New Relic license key.

## New Relic plan tiers

New Relic has four tiers of plans – Free, Standard, Pro, and Enterprise. To choose the right one for your site’s needs, check out and compare their plans on [New Relic’s pricing page](https://newrelic.com/pricing).

## Get a New Relic license key

If you haven’t already, sign up for a New Relic account. There are several API keys available for various features in New Relic. The key you’ll need to add to [MyKinsta](https://my.kinsta.com) is the one they refer to as the license key (aka ingest – license). This is the primary ingest key used for APM ingest and other data ingest.

Once you’re logged into your New Relic account, click **Copy license key**.

![](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/new-relic-copy-license-key.png)

If you don’t have this option, click your username > **API keys**, and copy the **INGEST – LICENSE** key.

![Copy New Relic license key.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/new-relic-copy-api-key.png)

Copy New Relic license key.

## Add New Relic monitoring in MyKinsta

To add your New Relic license key to MyKinsta, begin by logging in to [MyKinsta](https://my.kinsta.com/login). Go to your site’s **Tools** page and click **Enable** under **New Relic monitoring**.

![Enable New Relic monitoring for WordPress in MyKinsta.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/wordpress-tools-enable-new-relic.png)

Enable New Relic monitoring for WordPress in MyKinsta.

Enter your New Relic license key and click **Enable New Relic**.

![Add your New Relic license key in MyKinsta.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/wordpress-enable-new-relic-licence.png)

Add your New Relic license key in MyKinsta.

## Monitor your WordPress site with New Relic

After adding your license key in MyKinsta, you’ll be able to view performance data for your WordPress site in New Relic. If your data doesn’t show up after a few minutes, try logging out and logging back into your New Relic account.

If you do not see your site on the **All Entities** page in New Relic, click **APM & Services**. You should see your WordPress site. It will have the same name it has in MyKinsta.

![View APM & Services In New Relic.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/new-relic-apm-services.png)

Viewing APM & Services In New Relic.

Click on your site name, and you can see the associated APM data in New Relic.

![APM data in New Relic.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/new-relic-apm-data.png)

APM data in New Relic.

## Pause monitoring

If you pause New Relic monitoring, your license key is retained if you wish to resume it at a later time. When it is paused it does not have any impact on the performance of your site.

To pause New Relic monitoring, within **Sites** > ***sitename*** > **Tools** > **Change** > **Pause monitoring**.

![Pause New Relic monitoring.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/wordpress-new-relic-pause-monitoring.png)

Pause New Relic monitoring.

To confirm you want to pause monitoring, click **Pause New Relic**.

![Confirm you want to pause New Relic monitoring. ](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/wordpress-new-relic-confirm-pause.png)

Confirm you want to pause New Relic monitoring.

## Update your New Relic license key

To change your license key for New Relic, within **Sites** > ***sitename*** > **Tools**> **New Relic monitoring**, click **Change** > **Update license key**.

![Update your New Relic license key. ](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/wordpress-new-relic-update-license.png)

Update your New Relic license key.

Enter your new license key and click **Update license key**.

![Enter a new license key for New Relic monitoring. ](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/wordpress-new-relic-new-license.png)

Enter a new license key for New Relic monitoring.

## Disable New Relic monitoring

If you disable New Relic, this stops the monitoring on your site and removes the New Relic license key. If you only want to stop it without losing your license key, you can **Pause monitoring**.

To disable New Relic monitoring on your site, within **Sites** > ***sitename*** > **Tools**> **New Relic monitoring**, click **Change** > **Disable**.

![Disable New Relic monitoring.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/wordpress-new-relic-disable.png)

Disable New Relic monitoring.

To confirm you want to disable New Relic, click **Disable New Relic**.

![Confirm you want to disable New Relic monitoring. ](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/wordpress-new-relic-disable-confirm.png)

Confirm you want to disable New Relic monitoring.
