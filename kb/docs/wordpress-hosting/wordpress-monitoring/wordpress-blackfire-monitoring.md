[Blackfire.io](https://www.blackfire.io/monitoring/) is a monitoring tool that provides detailed information about your site’s performance in real-time to identify issues and optimize your site.

To use Blackfire monitoring, you’ll need to sign up for your own Blackfire account and set up an environment for your website hosted on MyKinsta.

## Enable Blackfire monitoring

Within MyKinsta, go to **Sites** > ***sitename*** > **Tools**, and within **Blackfire monitoring**, click **Enable**.

![Enable Blackfire monitoring on your site. ](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/wordpress-enable-blackfire.png)

Enable Blackfire monitoring on your site.

MyKinsta requires a Server ID and Token from Blackfire. Within Blackfire, select your environment, then go to **Settings** > **Environment credentials**.

![Blackfire environment credentials. ](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/blackfire-environment-credentials.png)

Blackfire environment credentials.

Copy and paste the **Server ID** and **Token** into MyKinsta, and click **Enable Blackfire**.

![Enter your Blackfire credentials into MyKinsta.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/wordpress-enable-blackfire-1.png)

Enter your Blackfire credentials into MyKinsta.

## Monitor your WordPress site with Blackfire

To view your performance data within Blackfire, select your environment and click **Monitoring**.

![View your performance data within Blackfire. ](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/blackfire-monitoring.png)

View your performance data within Blackfire.

## Pause monitoring

If you pause Blackfire monitoring, your credentials are retained if you wish to resume it at a later time. When it is paused, it does not have any impact on the performance of your site.

To pause Blackfire monitoring, within **Sites** > ***sitename*** > **Tools** > **Blackfire monitoring**, click **Change** > **Pause monitoring**.

![Pause Blackfire monitoring. ](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/wordpress-pause-blackfire.png)

Pause Blackfire monitoring.

To confirm you want to pause monitoring, click **Pause Blackfire**.

![Confirm you want to pause Blackfire monitoring.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/wordpress-pause-blackfire-confirm.png)

Confirm you want to pause Blackfire monitoring.

## Change your Blackfire credentials

To change your Server ID or Token for Blackfire, within **Sites** > ***sitename*** > **Tools** > **Blackfire monitoring**, click **Change** > **Update credentials**.

![Update your credentials for Blackfire monitoring. ](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/wordpress-update-blackfire-credentials.png)

Update your credentials for Blackfire monitoring.

Enter your new Server ID and Token and click Update credentials.

![Enter your new Server ID and Token for Blackfire. ](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/wordpress-update-blackfire-credentials-confirm.png)

Enter your new Server ID and Token for Blackfire.

## Disable Blackfire monitoring

If you disable Blackfire this stops the monitoring on your site and removes the Blackfire credentials. If you only want to stop it without losing your credentials, you can **Pause monitoring**.

To disable Blackfire monitoring on your site, within **Sites** > ***sitename*** > **Tools** > **Blackfire monitoring**, click **Change** > **Disable**.

![Disable Blackfire monitoring. ](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/wordpress-disable-blackfire.png)

Disable Blackfire monitoring.

To confirm you want to disable Blackfire, click **Disable Blackfire**.

![Confirm you want to disable Blackfire.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/wordpress-disable-blackfire-confirm.png)

Confirm you want to disable Blackfire.
