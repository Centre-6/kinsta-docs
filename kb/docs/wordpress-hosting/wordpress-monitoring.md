# WordPress Hosting - Monitoring

**Product:** Managed WordPress Hosting
**Source:** https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-monitoring/
**Kinsta last updated:** July 2, 2025

---

With Kinsta’s WordPress Hosting, you can monitor the performance of your sites to ensure you’re using the right resources, identify optimization opportunities, and investigate and diagnose site issues.

You can use [Kinsta’s APM tool](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-monitoring/apm-tool/) to help you identify [PHP performance](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/php/) bottlenecks on your WordPress site. You also have the option to enable integration with [Blackfire monitoring](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-monitoring/wordpress-blackfire-monitoring/) or [New Relic monitoring](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-monitoring/custom-new-relic-tracking/) to provide you with detailed performance breakdowns on a granular level.

This section also explains [how Kinsta monitors the uptime](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-monitoring/uptime-monitoring/) of all WordPress sites.
