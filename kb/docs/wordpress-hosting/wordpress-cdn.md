# WordPress Hosting - CDN

**Product:** Managed WordPress Hosting
**Source:** https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-cdn/
**Kinsta last updated:** November 6, 2025

---

The performance of your WordPress sites has and always will remain one of the top priorities for us. Because of that, we’ve [selected Cloudflare](https://kinsta.com/cloudflare-integration/) to offer enterprise-level firewall and DDoS protection, as well as a high-performance HTTP/3 CDN powered by Cloudflare’s robust global network. Free bandwidth is included for all Kinsta clients.

Kinsta’s CDN is enabled by default on all new sites here at Kinsta. If the CDN isn’t currently enabled for your WordPress site, check out [how to enable Kinsta’s CDN](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-cdn/kinsta-cdn/) in [MyKinsta](https://my.kinsta.com/).

### Third-party CDN providers

We also support [third-party CDN providers](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-cdn/proxy-cdn-compatibility-checklist/). Below are a few popular providers we recommend:

- [KeyCDN](https://keycdn.com)
- [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/) (you can use [your own Cloudflare account](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-cdn/cloudflare/) alongside our Cloudflare integration)
- [Fastly CDN](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-cdn/fastly-cdn/)
- [Amazon CloudFront](https://aws.amazon.com/cloudfront/)
- [Ezoic](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-cdn/ezoic/)
- [Imperva CDN](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-cdn/imperva-cdn/)
- [Sucuri](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-cdn/sucuri/)
- [Bunny](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-cdn/bunny/)
