Kinsta’s site labeling tool allows you to easily organize and filter your WordPress sites based on unique tags. This tool is especially useful for [WordPress agencies](https://kinsta.com/wordpress-hosting/agencies/) and users managing a large number of sites.

You can add or remove labels on the**Sites** page or an individual site’s **Info** page (**Sites** > ***sitename*** > **Info**). Site labels can be managed in **Company settings**, then **Site labels**.

### Label sites on the WordPress sites page

In [MyKinsta](https://my.kinsta.com) >**Sites**, select the checkboxes next to the site(s) you want to label, click **Actions**, and choose **Change labels**.

![Change labels for multiple sites simultaneously.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/wordpress-sites-change-labels-2.png)

Change labels for multiple sites simultaneously.

Click **Add new label** to create a new label. Enter the new label and click **Apply labels**.

If you have existing labels you want to manage, you can click **Manage all labels in company settings**.

![Add a new site label.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/wordpress-add-label-1024x333.png)

Add a new site label.

After a label is created, it will always be available for use in the future. The screenshot below shows “Label 1”, the label we created above.

To remove a label from a site, in the **Label sites** modal/pop-up, deselect the box next to the label you want to remove and click **Apply labels**.

![Label 1 added in MyKinsta.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/wordpress-remove-label-1024x386.png)

Label 1 added in MyKinsta.

After labeling your sites, you can filter the site list based on a specific label.

![Filter by site label in MyKinsta.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/wordpress-sites-label-list.png)

Filter by site label in MyKinsta.

### Label sites on the Site Information page

Another way to view, add, or remove labels is in the **Basic details** of an individual **Site Information** page (**Sites** > ***sitename*** > **Info**). Click the pencil (edit) icon in the **Labels** field.

![Click the pencil (edit) icon on Labels to edit labels.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/wordpress-site-info-labels.png)

Click the pencil (edit) icon on Labels to edit labels.

Click **Add new label** to create a new label. If you have existing labels you want to manage, you can click **Manage all labels in company settings**.

![Add a new site label.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/wordpress-add-label-1024x333.png)

Add a new site label.

After a label is created, it will always be available for use in the future. The screenshot below shows “Label 1”, the label we created above.

To remove a label from a site, in the **Label sites** modal/pop-up, deselect the box next to the label you want to remove and click **Apply labels**.

![Label 1 added in MyKinsta.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/wordpress-remove-label-1024x386.png)

Label 1 added in MyKinsta.

After adding labels to your site, you can see them in the **Labels** field.

![Site labels on the Site Information page in MyKinsta.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/wordpress-labels-added.png)

Site labels on the Site Information page in MyKinsta.

### Manage site labels

To manage your site labels, click **Company settings** > **Site Labels**. You can also click the **Manage all labels in Company settings** link in the Label sites modal.

Here you can add, rename, or delete labels.

To add a new label, click the **Add new label** button.

![Add a new site label.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/wordpress-site-labels.png)

Add a new site label.

To rename a label, click the pencil (edit) icon on the right-hand side.

![Click the pencil (edit) icon in MyKinsta to change a label name.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/wordpress-site-labels-edit.png)

Click the pencil (edit) icon in MyKinsta to change a label name.

To delete a label, click the trash can (delete) icon on the right-hand side.

![Click the trash can (delete) icon to delete a site label.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/wordpress-site-labels-delete.png)

Click the trash can (delete) icon to delete a site label.

After a site has been labeled, the label will be visible in your site list.

![Site labels under site names in MyKinsta.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/wordpress-labelled-sites.png)

Site labels under site names in MyKinsta.
