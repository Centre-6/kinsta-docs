A dedicated server gives you a virtual server reserved entirely for your sites. Instead of running on standard infrastructure sized for typical workloads, you choose the CPU, RAM, and storage your WordPress sites actually need, and we provision a server built to match. This makes dedicated servers a good fit for high-traffic sites, resource-intensive applications, or organizations with specific compliance or performance requirements.

## Benefits of a dedicated server

You get all the regular benefits Kinsta offers, plus:

- **Dedicated resources:** Ample CPU and RAM to handle high-traffic sites and complex applications.
- **Greater control:** Flexibility to make changes, such as disabling homepage caching or adjusting PHP values.
- **Custom configurations:** Support for settings beyond standard limits, like increasing memory above 512 MB or running cron jobs every minute.
- **Improved compliance and data privacy:** Hosted on SOC 2–certified infrastructure, well suited to large corporations and financial institutions.
- **Long-term reliability:** Consistent performance, security, and stability for organizations that depend on it.

## How much does a dedicated server cost?

The short answer is, it depends. Pricing starts at $300 a month but varies depending on:

- Resources needed for your site (CPU, RAM, Storage).
- Amount of traffic expected.
- The data center location where your site is hosted.

Our team will need this information, along with some other details, to provide a recommendation for a dedicated server.

## Important dedicated server information

- The dedicated server subscription is separate from your hosting plan and **is non-refundable in the first 30 days**. If you cancel your dedicated server after the first 30 days, you will receive a prorated [credit](https://kinsta.com/docs/billing/account-credits/) for the dedicated server fee (not the WordPress Hosting plan) to your Account Balance for the remaining days in your current billing period. For more information, refer to [Kinsta’s WordPress Hosting Money-Back Guarantee](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/money-back-guarantee/).
- The dedicated server subscription automatically renews and remains active and billable until you [contact our Account Management team](https://kinsta.com/contact-us/) to cancel it.
- Your current hosting plan at Kinsta needs to be active and have enough visits and disk space for all sites on your plan.
- **If you anticipate an increase in traffic, and this is your reason for purchasing a dedicated server, ensure your current plan is large enough for that increase. If not, you’ll need to upgrade your plan first.**For example, if you’re currently on a Single 125k plan (125,000 visits/month) and expect your site to receive 1,000,000 visitors, you’ll want to upgrade to at least a Single 1.25M plan that supports 1,250,000 visits/month.
- Our team’s recommendations are based purely on the data provided and from visiting the site. We do not provide consultation services on how to [improve performance](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-troubleshooting/troubleshooting-wordpress-performance/) or find bottlenecks.
- Adding a dedicated server to an account does not ensure the site will support the intended traffic. Consideration should still be made for how well the [site caches](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/caching/), how well-optimized the code is, and how much traffic is expected.
- When you create a [staging environment](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/staging-environment/) (premium or standard) on a dedicated server, it shares the same resources as the live site. The resources for the staging environments can be adjusted to avoid causing issues with the live site. [Contact our Support team](https://kinsta.com/docs/support/contact-support/) to check the resources on your server, and we will adjust the staging environment resources if required.
- You can request a server with greater or fewer resources based on how you prefer to move forward.

## Dedicated server checklist

- Is the dedicated server for normal traffic or to handle an increase in traffic during a short-term event?
- If the dedicated server is for a short-term event, when will the event start and end?
- What is the URL of the site?
- Does most incoming traffic arrive at a specific page? If so, what page?
- What is the maximum number of concurrent users you expect during peak usage?
- What average page view duration do you expect during peak usage (“Average time on page” in Google Analytics)?
- What is the average number of page views per visitor you expect during peak usage?
- How would you describe your site? Is it primarily an ecommerce site? Do you offer an online course? Is it a membership site? Is it a news website?
- How will a typical visitor use the site? For example, will they reach a landing page, select a product, and check out? Will they log into the website, select a course, and complete the course by watching videos and reading copy, or something else?
- If your site is an ecommerce site, requires users to log in, or allows visitors to post content other than comments (like a forum, for example), what plugins are you using for these interactive features (e.g. WooCommerce, BuddyPress, bbPress, Easy Digital Downloads, LearnDash, etc.)?
- How many [staging environments](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/staging-environment/) do you require? You can have one standard staging environment and up to five premium staging environments. On a dedicated server, the live and staging environments share the same resources.
- Is your site a standard WordPress installation, or is it a [multisite installation](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-getting-started/multisite-network/)?

If there’s any additional information you think we should know, please include that with your request.

## How to purchase a dedicated server

Gather the information requested in the previous section. A dedicated server takes time to provision and configure, so you must obtain the correct information so we can start the process as early as possible.

To purchase a dedicated server, in MyKinsta, select your site, go to the **Add-ons** page, and click **Request add-on** under **Dedicated server**.

![Request a dedicated server add-on.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/wordpress-request-dedi-add-on.png)

Request a dedicated server add-on.

In the modal that opens, tell us what resources you need, then click **Send request** to start a chat with our Support team, who’ll be in touch to provide a quote for your dedicated server.

![Send us the requirements of your dedicated server.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/wordpress-request-dedi.png)

Send us the requirements of your dedicated server.

After you approve the quote and pay for the first month of service, our team will need up to 3 business days to configure the dedicated server. Once it’s ready, our team will coordinate with you to move your site container to the dedicated server.

## Request changes to the dedicated server

Once your dedicated server is set up, if you need any changes or want to cancel it and move your sites back to our standard infrastructure, go to the **Add-ons** page and click **Change** under **Dedicated server**.
