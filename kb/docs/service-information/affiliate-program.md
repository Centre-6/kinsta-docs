If you’re a [Kinsta customer](https://kinsta.com/case-studies/) already or simply an online entrepreneur looking to diversify your income streams, joining the Kinsta affiliate program is a great way to earn extra money while recommending top-tier hosting.

As a Kinsta affiliate, for each WordPress-referred customer, you’ll receive both of the following:

- A one-time commission between $50 and $500
- A monthly recurring commission of 10%

## Why you should join Kinsta’s Affiliate program

Here are a few reasons why you may want to join Kinsta’s affiliate program.

- Kinsta’s churn rate is less than 4%, which means the majority of our clients love hosting on Kinsta. You won’t have to worry about losing out on recurring revenue due to poor performance and service on Kinsta.
- Kinsta uses high-performance, enterprise-grade infrastructure to power every Managed WordPress site. Our platform is built on a global network of premium data centers, designed to deliver exceptional speed, stability, and scalability. On some sites, we’ve seen a [+200% increase in performance](https://kinsta.com/blog/wordpress-performance/) after moving to Kinsta.
- Our custom-built dashboard has everything you need to manage a site with ease – free SSLs, premium DNS, password protection, redirects, and more. Schedule a [MyKinsta demo](https://kinsta.com/talk-to-sales/) to learn more about our dashboard features.
- With 30 data centers around the world, your referrals can choose a location that works for them.

Our [industry-leading support team](https://kinsta.com/kinsta-support/) is available 24/7, 365 days a year. Your referrals will be in good hands!

## How to join Kinsta’s Affiliate program

Getting started as a Kinsta affiliate is a three-step process.

### Register as an affiliate

To sign up as a Kinsta affiliate, visit our [affiliate portal](https://affiliate.kinsta.com/register). After submitting your details, a member of our team will get in touch with you to review your application.

![Register as a Kinsta affiliate.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/affiliate-register.png)

Register as a Kinsta affiliate.

### Create your affiliate links

Create your affiliate links and download our banners to share on your sites and social media. We offer assets in a variety of colors, styles, and sizes.

To create an affiliate link, log in to the [affiliate dashboard](https://affiliate.kinsta.com/login), on the bottom left click **Create affiliate link,** paste any URL you’d like to link to from the Kinsta website, and click **Create link**.

![Create affiliate links](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/affiliate-create-link.png)

Create affiliate links

You can use any page or blog post on the Kinsta website, such as:

- Kinsta homepage: `https://kinsta.com/`
- Kinsta features page: `https://kinsta.com/wordpress-hosting/`
- Kinsta plans pages `https://kinsta.com/pricing/`
- Kinsta “Speed Up WordPress” guide: `https://kinsta.com/learn/speed-up-wordpress/`

You can then copy the affiliate link generated with your affiliate ID. You can then use this link on your website, in an email, on a banner, or anywhere you like. When someone clicks on your link, it places a cookie in their browser that lasts for 60 days. You will then get credited for the sale when anyone purchases a Kinsta plan within that time.

![Affiliate links to use on your website. ](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/affiliate-links.png)

Affiliate links to use on your website.

The links created have the **sponsored** attribute to comply with Google’s guidelines. If you are using a third-party affiliate plugin like [Pretty Links](https://prettylinks.com/blog/nofollow-your-affiliate-links/), they have easy ways to add the sponsored attribute to all of your affiliate links.

### Earn commissions on referrals

As soon as your links convert, you’ll be credited with the referral commission right in your PayPal account. These will be shown within **Payments**.

## Affiliate dashboard

The affiliate dashboard shows you an overview of your referrals and payments.

![Affiliate dashboard.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/affiliate-dashboard-1.png)

Affiliate dashboard.

- **Active referrals:** The total number of referred customers. These customers have used your affiliate link to register a Kinsta account and create a new MyKinsta company.
- **Estimated next payment:** The estimated total amount of commission you may receive in your next payment from Kinsta.
- **Total payments to date:** The total amount of commission you have earned from referrals.
- **Referred page views:** The total number of times your affiliate links have been clicked.

The charts on the dashboard show the following information:

- **Referred page views:** The number of times the affiliate links have been clicked for the past 7 days.
- **Referred visitors:**The number of visitors that have clicked on the affiliate links for the past 7 days. This number can differ from the page views as the same visitor may click on a link more than once.
- **Daily referrals:**The number of customers that have used your affiliate link to register a Kinsta account and create a new MyKinsta company for the past 7 days.
- **Latest referrals:** The latest customers that have used your affiliate link to register a Kinsta account and create a new MyKinsta company.
- **Latest approved commissions:** A commission is approved when it meets all of the following conditions:
  - The referred customer has never previously been a customer of Kinsta;
  - The referred customer has made payment to Kinsta for the services;
  - The affiliate’s account is active at the time the referred customer pays its fees to Kinsta; and
  - Kinsta has received all requested tax documents (e.g., Form W-9) from the affiliate.

For more information about referrals and commissions, refer to the [Affiliate FAQs](https://kinsta.com/affiliates/faq/) or the [Affiliate Program Terms and Conditions](https://kinsta.com/legal/affiliate-terms/).

## Referred customers

Referred customers is a list of all customers that have used your affiliate link to register a Kinsta account and create a new MyKinsta company.

![A list of referred customers.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/affiliate-referred-customers.png)

A list of referred customers.

- **ID:** A unique ID for the customer referral.
- **Referred on:** The date the customer was referred.
- **Plan:**The WordPress Hosting plan the customer has signed up for.
- **One-time commission:** This shows the status of the commission, which can be one of the following:
  - **Accumulating:** There’s a 2-month qualification period for referrals of Managed WordPress Hosting to become certified. This window ends when your referred customer has had an active WordPress hosting subscription for 2 months and settled all payments. The monthly recurring commissions will start accruing once the one-time commission has been approved, and the accumulating message will change to the amount of the one-time commission.
  - **$50 – $500:** This is the one-time commission amount you will receive, which depends on the plan the customer purchases.
  - **Cancelled before 60 days:** This shows if the customer canceled the subscription or never subscribed to a paid plan.

For more information about referrals and commissions, refer to the [Affiliate FAQs](https://kinsta.com/affiliates/faq/) or the [Affiliate Program Terms and Conditions](https://kinsta.com/legal/affiliate-terms/).
