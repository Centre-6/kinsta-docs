# Service Information - System Status

**Product:** Service Information
**Source:** https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/status-page/
**Kinsta last updated:** July 18, 2024

---

You can access our [status page](https://status.kinsta.com/), showing the operational status for all data centers, our website, and MyKinsta.

## Subscribe to status updates

You can subscribe to status updates by clicking **Subscribe to Updates** on the [status page](https://status.kinsta.com/).

## Unsubscribe from status updates

To unsubscribe from status updates:

- **Email notifications**: On the [status page](https://status.kinsta.com/), click **Subscribe to Updates**, enter your email address, click **Subscribe via Email** > **Unsubscribe from Updates** > **Unsubscribe from Updates**.
- **Text message notifications**: [Contact the Kinsta support team](https://kinsta.com/docs/support/contact-support/).
