## Open a support chat

To open a new support chat or ticket, log in to [MyKinsta](https://my.kinsta.com/ "Kinsta Login") and click the **chat icon** in the lower right corner.

![Contacting Support in MyKinsta.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/wordpress-opem-support-chat.png)

Contacting Support in MyKinsta.

Click the **Need help?** button or choose to chat with the AI assistant by clicking **Chat with Kinsta’s AI Assistant**.

![Send a message in MyKinsta.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/wordpress-contact-support.png)

Send a message in MyKinsta.

Choose the type of support you need:

![Choose the type of support you need. ](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/wordpress-contact-support-options.png)

Choose the type of support you need.

If you choose Technical Support, you then need to choose which sites you need help with:

![Select your sites.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/wordpress-contact-support-sites-1.png)

Select your sites.

Once you’ve selected the sites, you can enter your message to support and send it. Please add as much information as possible.

![Describe your issue for our support team. ](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/wordpress-contact-support-info.png)

Describe your issue for our support team.

If you choose any other option, you can type your message, and it will be routed to the appropriate team.

![Enter your message for the required Kinsta team.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/wordpress-contact-support-email.png)

Enter your message for the required Kinsta team.

Our Support team’s average response time is **under 2 minutes**!

### Switch chats

When you open the chat window and click **Send us a message**, a new chat opens. If you have an ongoing query with the support team, you can switch chats to reopen and continue an existing conversation.

In the chat window, click the back arrow.

![Click back in the chat window to open an existing conversation.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/wordpress-contact-support-back.png)

Click back in the chat window to open an existing conversation.

Click **Messages** to view all previous chats, and then click on the chat you want to continue or reopen.

![View all of your previous chats with the support team.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/wordpress-contact-support-messages.png)

View all of your previous chats with the support team.

### Save a chat transcript

To download a transcript of your conversation, scroll to the top of the window and click the ellipsis (three dots), and then click **Download transcript**. A pop-up window will appear with a .txt file of your transcript that you can save to your computer.

![Save a transcript of your chat.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/wordpress-contact-support-TRANSCRIPT.png)

Save a transcript of your chat.

## Multilingual support and hours

At Kinsta, we are proud to offer multilingual support, not only for support tickets but also for sales, MyKinsta, our affiliate program, and our website. Below, we’ll answer some common questions about our support hours, the Billing team, the languages we support, and where.

**We are continuously expanding the languages we support across all of the services and tools on our platform.**

### Hosting support languages and hours

Unlike many other hosting providers, we don’t have level 1 or level 2 support representatives. Our entire [support team is made up of highly skilled WordPress developers](https://kinsta.com/kinsta-support/) and Linux engineers. Many contribute to WordPress core, open-source projects, and even develop plugins. Every Kinsta client gets to chat with the same team members who support our large enterprise and Fortune 500 companies. As a result, our support department’s expertise is second to none.

You can open a new support ticket with our team at any time from the bottom-right corner of MyKinsta.

## Why we don’t offer phone support

**Kinsta offers 24/7 support via our chat system**, which is always one click away in [MyKinsta](https://my.kinsta.com/) for customers with a paid plan. Our team of engineers are developers who contribute to WordPress core, and use WordPress on a daily basis. As a result, the [expertise of our Support team is second to none](https://kinsta.com/kinsta-support/). You’ll always have a knowledgeable team member on the other end.

Support work often requires careful, focused thought and investigation. Keeping support solely online allows us to better help you troubleshoot your website more quickly and efficiently. Our engineers can focus all their energy on resolving your support concerns with the fewest distractions and interruptions. This, in turn, means that your **support requests are ultimately resolved faster**. We believe it wouldn’t be possible to provide the same effective assistance over the phone.

Here are a few additional reasons why:

- A chat lets us immediately pull up your customer information, so we **don’t need to spend time figuring out who you are**.
- Our system allows for **two-way sharing of screenshots, links, code snippets, and videos** of problems you are having. This ensures everyone is on the same page and, in turn, helps our Support team pinpoint the issues faster.
- We can automatically gather technical information for research and troubleshooting purposes and **link you directly** to our online [documentation](https://kinsta.com/docs/), which we are constantly improving.
- All support chats and conversations are stored for access at all times. This allows anyone on our team to pick up where another left off **without having to ask you questions twice**.

By focusing on online support channels, we can scale our support and dedicate resources to continually improve and innovate our all-in-one hosting platform. But don’t just take our word for it; [check out feedback from happy Kinsta clients](https://kinsta.com/kinsta-reviews/).
