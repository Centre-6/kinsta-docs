Our [Support team](https://kinsta.com/kinsta-support/) is available to assist you 24/7. [Support is provided exclusively through a chat system](https://kinsta.com/docs/support/contact-support/#open-a-support-chat) integrated into [MyKinsta](https://my.kinsta.com). To reach our Support team, use the chat icon in the lower right-hand corner of MyKinsta at any time. In this article, we’ll explain how our Support team can assist you as well as a few limitations that do apply.

For more details about what support we offer, refer to [Scope of Support — WordPress Hosting](https://kinsta.com/docs/support/scope-of-support/managed-wordpress-scope-of-support/).

## Getting help fast

While our average initial response time is less than two minutes, you can get help even faster by searching our documentation. If you have questions about how to use any of our hosting features, our documentation is the best place to start.

## If in doubt, don’t hesitate to ask

We’re here to help. So if you aren’t sure if a specific issue is covered by our Support team, don’t hesitate to open up a chat and ask!

## Feedback and testing opportunities

Kinsta occasionally offers opportunities to [test and provide feedback](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/kinsta-research-program/) on new features and products. Due to the nature of beta tests, investigation and communication via email may take place across several weekdays. As a reminder, beta features should be used with caution and are not intended for production use.
