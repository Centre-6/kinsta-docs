At Kinsta, we’re proud to offer discounts on [Managed WordPress Hosting](https://kinsta.com/wordpress-hosting/) plans to open-source projects and nonprofit organizations. Additionally, when you sign up or upgrade to an [annual plan](https://kinsta.com/pricing/), you’ll automatically receive **2 months free**!

## Open source

To receive a 15% discount for your open-source project, send your website and details about your project to our [Sales team](https://kinsta.com/contact-us/). We’ll confirm your project’s status and apply the discount to all of your future payments.

If you sign up for or switch to an annual plan, we give you 2 months of free service on top of the 15% discount.

Note: To qualify for the discount, the open-source site must have its own separate Managed WordPress Hosting account. The discount applies exclusively to the WordPress Hosting plan and does not extend to any add-ons.

## Nonprofit

Nonprofit organizations can also receive a discount on Managed WordPress Hosting. We offer a 15% discount on [web hosting for nonprofits](https://kinsta.com/wordpress-hosting/nonprofits/), including charities, churches, 501(c)3 organizations, and similar groups. To confirm eligibility, please provide our Sales team with a copy of your 501(c)3 or the equivalent documentation for your country. Once verified, the discount will be applied to all future payments.

If you sign up for or switch to an annual plan, we give you 2 months of free service on top of the 15% discount.

Note: The nonprofit site needs to have its own separate hosting account to qualify for the discount. The discount applies exclusively to the WordPress Hosting plan and does not extend to any add-ons.

## Annual term

For all customers, you can pay yearly on any Managed WordPress Hosting plan and [get two months free](https://kinsta.com/pricing/)!

If you sign up for Single 35k visits, Single 20GB bandwidth, WP 2, or Launch plan, the first month is free for both monthly and annual plans; this means you don’t pay anything for the first month and still get another two months free if you pay annually. For example, if you choose a WP 2 annual plan on April 1, 2025, you don’t pay anything on that date. On May 1, 2025, you are charged the annual plan amount, which includes the 2 months free, and your plan renews as normal on April 1, 2026.

You can [switch to an annual plan](https://kinsta.com/docs/billing/wordpress-hosting-plans/#switch-your-plan) with just a few clicks from your MyKinsta dashboard. Additionally, our system calculates the unused time on your monthly plan and prorates the charge accordingly.
