# Billing Details

**Section:** Billing
**Product:** Billing

---

In this article, you’ll learn how to update your billing address in [MyKinsta](https://my.kinsta.com/). Updating the billing address associated with a company is only available for users with [Company Administrator](https://kinsta.com/docs/company-settings/user-management/#company-administrator) and [Company Billing](https://kinsta.com/docs/company-settings/user-management/#company-billing) roles.

## Edit billing details

To edit your billing information, from the **Dashboard**, click **Company settings** and then **Billing Details**. This is where you can update your billing address. When you’re finished, click **Save Changes** to confirm the changes.
