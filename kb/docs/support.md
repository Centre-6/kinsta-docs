# Support

**Product:** Support
**Source:** https://kinsta.com/docs/support/
**Kinsta last updated:** February 2, 2026

---

[Support is provided exclusively through a chat system](https://kinsta.com/docs/support/contact-support/#open-a-support-chat) integrated into [MyKinsta](https://my.kinsta.com/). To reach our Support team, use the chat icon in the lower right-hand corner of MyKinsta at any time. You can also connect with developers and knowledgeable community members in the [Kinsta Community forum](https://community.kinsta.com/).

For more details about what support we offer, refer to [Scope of Support — Managed WordPress Hosting](https://kinsta.com/docs/support/scope-of-support/managed-wordpress-scope-of-support/).

Support is available 24/7 in all languages supported in MyKinsta, to find out how to contact support, refer to [Contact Support](https://kinsta.com/docs/support/contact-support/).
