We are happy to announce that Kinsta is attending **[Web Summit](https://websummit.com/)** from **November 10-13** at the **MEO Arena in Lisbon**. As one of Europe’s largest tech events, Web Summit brings together global tech leaders and new voices to connect and shape the future of technology.

It’s the place where ambitious founders connect with investors, customers, developers, and industry leaders, and it provides them with the opportunity to turn their ideas into reality. We’ll be there to show how Kinsta can help bring your projects to life. We can’t wait to see you there!

We strongly recommend planning your [agenda](https://websummit.com/schedule/) before the conference starts, as these four days are packed with keynotes and panels from tech giants, curated networking events, masterclasses, meetups, and more. And be sure to set aside time to visit our booth, meet our team, and discover how our hosting service helps sites grow.

## Meet the Kinsta team in person

If you’re attending Web Summit, come by booth E311 to meet our official representatives:

- [**Marcel Bootsman**](https://nl.linkedin.com/in/marcelbootsman) – Partnerships & Community Manager EMEA
- [**Leah Tobin**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/leah-tobin-0bb13b4b/) – Business Development Representative
- [**Lauren Perry-Carrera**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lauren-perry-carrera/) – Business Development Representative
- [**Sam Gooch**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/samuelgooch/) – Head of Performance Marketing
- [**Pedro Oliveira**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pedromnoliveira/) – Full-Stack Web Designer

![The Kinsta team at Web Summit Lisbon](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/websummit-lisbon-team-announcement-dark.png)

The Kinsta team at Web Summit Lisbon

Our team will be there in full force to discuss WordPress, covering topics such as performance and scalability, developer tools, and best practices. Stop by to ask questions about hosting and learn how Kinsta’s **[Managed Hosting for WordPress](https://kinsta.com/wordpress-hosting/)** can help you build faster, streamline workflows, and confidently scale your projects.

We’re also bringing some fun swag to the event, so stop by our booth early to pick up some Kinsta swag, available while supplies last.

## Let’s meet in Lisbon

We’re excited to see you at Web Summit! Be sure to visit Kinsta’s booth to meet our representatives and pick up some swag. It’s also the perfect opportunity to chat with our team, connect with the community, and discover how partnering with Kinsta can elevate your WordPress projects.
