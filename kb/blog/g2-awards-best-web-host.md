## The need for speed: Performance plus reliability

Are we ever satisfied that our websites are “fast enough?” Not really. But here at Kinsta, our engineering team and system operators work tirelessly to continually improve the performance of our hosting platform. They’re always striving for more.

Performance encompasses more than just speed. Our customers require mission-critical websites to be up and running all the time.

[Calvin B.](https://www.g2.com/products/kinsta/reviews/kinsta-review-12205915) describes it for G2 like this:

> I love Kinsta’s reliability, high performance, and seamless scalability. I’ve never experienced downtime in seven years, which is invaluable for client websites.

[Chris M.](https://www.g2.com/products/kinsta/reviews/kinsta-review-12244445) also needs speed and reliability:

> The platform is fast and stable, and it keeps improving without me having to intervene or rework client setups. For high-traffic WooCommerce sites, that reliability is critical.

[Michael F.](https://www.g2.com/products/kinsta/reviews/kinsta-review-11197951) knows what it’s like when things go wrong elsewhere:

> Kinsta has completely solved the performance issues we faced with WooCommerce on high-traffic days. Their server architecture handles PHP workers far better than any host we’ve used before, which is critical for dynamic eCommerce sites like ours. Our previous hosting provider couldn’t handle simultaneous user sessions without serious slowdowns — with Kinsta, everything runs smoothly even during peak loads.

Kinsta makes it easy for WordPress customers to fine-tune the performance of individual sites, balancing available memory with PHP threads (or workers) to meet unique traffic profiles.

![A screenshot of the PHP Performance tool in MyKinsta.](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/php-performace-2g-512mb.jpg)

Adjusting the memory pool and PHP thread count in the MyKinsta dashboard.

And between customers’ optimized servers and their own site visitors, Kinsta’s [Cloudflare integration](https://kinsta.com/blog/cloudflare-integration/) further boosts performance with CDN and edge caching through 300+ data centers worldwide.
