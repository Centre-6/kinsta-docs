If you want to build long-term relationships with your agency’s clients, hosting their websites is a great service to offer.

When things are going well, hosting your clients’ sites lets you build a [predictable source of recurring revenue](https://kinsta.com/blog/build-recurring-revenue-into-agency/), while also putting your agency first in line if clients ever need additional work. But it’s a problem when things are *not* going well with hosting your clients’ sites. Issues such as downtime, slow performance, security breaches, and inadequate support can deliver poor results for your clients, negatively impact client relationships (or even cause them to churn), and force your team to spend time administering hosting servers instead of performing more product tasks.

Below, we explain why a lack of hosting stability can undermine the goal of building long-term client relationships. ## Why hosting instability is the hidden threat to agency-client relationships

If a client chooses to turn a one-time project into an ongoing hosting relationship, they’ve probably had a good experience with your agency so far. After all, if they weren’t satisfied with what you offer, they probably would have moved in a different direction after the initial work.

If you want to continue nurturing that positive, long-term relationship with your agency, it’s essential to offer fast and reliable hosting for your clients’ websites.

Unfortunately, hosting instability can easily turn a previously positive client relationship into a negative one. This can lead to issues with client satisfaction and increase the churn rate of your agency’s clients.

Hosting instability can manifest in different ways, all of which can be detrimental to client relationships:

- **Downtime:** [Website downtime](https://kinsta.com/blog/website-downtime/), especially at an important time, will lead to client dissatisfaction. Downtime can damage a client’s reputation and cause them to miss revenue or leads.
- **Slow load times:** Even if there’s no downtime, [performance issues](https://kinsta.com/blog/wordpress-slow/) can still be frustrating for clients. Slow load times degrade customer experience and can lead to lower conversion rates (which also means [lost revenue for your clients](https://kinsta.com/blog/wordpress-performance/)). On the backend, clients might also feel frustrated when managing content in their website dashboards, which can lead to more client dissatisfaction.
- **Security issues:** [Security issues](https://kinsta.com/blog/wordpress-security/) cause the same problems as downtime and also open up even more reputational risk for clients. This is especially true if the security breach leads to leaked customer data, and clients face the unenviable task of notifying their customers about the issue.
- **Support problems:** While clients might not interact directly with your hosting provider’s support, issues with that support can damage client relationships. If it takes too long for your team to get results from support, your clients can feel the impact.

These types of instability aren’t just problematic for the immediate issues that they cause for client relationships. Every second your agency wastes on dealing with website downtime, performance, and security is also time that you and your team can’t spend on more productive tasks.
