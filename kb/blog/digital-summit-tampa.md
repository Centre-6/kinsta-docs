We’re thrilled to share that Kinsta is heading to sunny Florida to sponsor [**Digital Summit Tampa 2026**](https://www.digitalsummit.com/tampa). We’re looking forward to meeting you at the Hilton Tampa Downtown on **March 17-18** for expert-led sessions, insightful workshops, and valuable networking opportunities.

Digital Summit events are known for bringing together forward-thinking marketing professionals and industry changemakers, creating the perfect environment to exchange ideas and build meaningful connections. Attendees can deepen their expertise, explore the latest industry trends, and develop new skills that help drive business growth and stronger digital strategies.

With [sessions](https://www.digitalsummit.com/tampa/agenda) covering topics like SEO, AI, analytics, and digital strategy, Digital Summit Tampa offers practical insights marketing professionals can apply immediately.

## Meet the Kinsta team and catch us on stage

If you’re attending Digital Summit Tampa, come meet the Kinsta team at our booth. We’ll have team members joining from across departments, including:

- [**Roger Williams**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rogerwilliamsmedia) – Partnerships and Community Manager, North America
- [**Kate Orchard**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kate-orchard/) – Product Marketing Manager
- [**Natalie Bessen**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nataliebessenclientspecialist) – Account Executive

![The Kinsta team at Digital Summit Tampa](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/digital-summit-tampa-team-announcement-light.png)

The Kinsta team at Digital Summit Tampa

Whether you’re looking to optimize performance, improve site reliability, or scale your digital projects, our team will be happy to talk about how Kinsta can support your goals.

We’re excited that Roger Williams, Kinsta’s Partnerships and Community Manager for North America, will take the stage at Digital Summit Tampa. In his session, “***The Steady Hand: Building the Next-Gen Marketing Stack on Proven Foundations***,” on **March 17, from 11:30 a.m. to 12 p.m.**, Roger will discuss how organizations can combine the proven reliability of open-source technologies like WordPress, PHP, and MariaDB with the expertise of experienced developers and agencies to build systems that are both innovative and resilient.
