We’re thrilled to share that Kinsta is heading back to Singapore this October! From **October 12 to 15,** our team will be in town for the [Hypergrowth Summit](https://hypergrowthsummit.asia/) and a series of exclusive networking dinners.

Whether you’re looking for hosting advice, exploring agency partnerships, or simply hoping to catch up with the WordPress community, we’d love to meet you.

Representing Kinsta throughout the week will be:

- **[Alex Ando-Michaelson](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alexandermichaelson/)** – Partnerships & Community Manager, APAC
- **[Gabrielius Vaičaitis](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gabisvaicaitis/)** – Head of Sales, EMEA/APAC

Here is where you can connect with us:

- [**October 12**: State of Digital Publishing private dinner](#state-of-digital-publishing-private-dinner)

## State of Digital Publishing private dinner

###### Monday, October 12

We’ll kick off the week by co-hosting **[The Audience Advantage — Publisher & Brand Dinner Singapore 2026](https://www.stateofdigitalpublishing.com/audience-advantage-singapore-2026/)** with State of Digital Publishing. This invitation-only evening will bring together 14 publishing and brand leaders from Singapore and Southeast Asia for a candid discussion about building stronger audience relationships and turning those connections into lasting business growth.

The evening centers on the theme “*The Audience Advantage: Turning Owned Audiences into Growth in the AI Era.*” Over dinner, guests will exchange perspectives on building direct audience relationships, putting first-party data and AI to work, and supporting growth with fast, reliable websites. With no panels or sales pitches, the focus will be on an open, off-the-record conversation about what’s working and where to invest next.

Interested in joining the discussion? Visit the [event page](https://www.stateofdigitalpublishing.com/audience-advantage-singapore-2026/) to apply for one of the limited seats.

## Kinsta’s Singapore Social with Chimera Collective

###### Tuesday, October 13

Next, we’re teaming up with our community partner, [Chimera Collective](https://chimeracreatives.com/), for an invite-only networking dinner to reconnect with familiar faces and welcome new ones. This event brings together agency and industry leaders from the WordPress and open web community for a relaxed evening of good food and conversation.

![Networking dinner in Singapore](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/singapore-partner-networking-event-white.png)

Networking dinner in Singapore.

We’re looking forward to hearing what our partners and peers are working on, sharing ideas, and getting to know the people behind the projects.

If you’re interested in joining us, feel free to reach out to the Kinsta team to request an invitation.

## Hypergrowth Summit

###### Thursday, October 15

We’ll round out the week as a proud sponsor of [Hypergrowth Summit](https://hypergrowthsummit.asia/), joining agency founders, marketing teams, and business leaders to explore practical approaches to growth across Southeast Asia. The program focuses on expanding into regional markets, combining human expertise with AI, and building stronger partnerships between agencies and businesses.

Stop by our booth on Thursday to meet our team and chat about hosting, website performance, and agency partnerships. Whether you’re managing a growing portfolio of client sites or planning your next digital project, bring your questions. We’d love to hear what you’re working on and explore how Kinsta can help. And don’t leave without picking up some cool Kinsta swag!

If you’ve purchased the VIP Experience, join us at the Welcome Dinner Reception on October 14 and the After Party on October 15 for more opportunities to connect beyond the conference floor.

Still need a ticket? Use promo code `KINSTAGOHSG26` to get $50 off your Hypergrowth Summit ticket.

## Let’s connect in Singapore

From small-group dinners to conversations between summit sessions, we’re looking forward to spending time with Singapore’s publishing, agency, marketing, and WordPress communities.

Joining us at Hypergrowth Summit or taking a seat at one of our private dinners? Come say hello, tell us about your next challenge, and discover how Kinsta can support your website, team, or business.
