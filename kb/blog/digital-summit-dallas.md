Kinsta is heading to Dallas, Texas, this December! As a sponsor of [**Digital Summit Dallas 2025**](https://www.digitalsummit.com/dallas), we’re excited to join marketers, creators, and digital leaders from the Dallas area and beyond on **December 3-4 at the Irving Convention Center**.

With inspiring expert-led sessions, in-depth masterclasses, workshops, and countless opportunities to network with professionals, Digital Summit Dallas offers practical takeaways you can put to work right away. Be sure to check the full [agenda](https://www.digitalsummit.com/dallas/agenda) in advance so you don’t miss anything.

## The Kinsta team

If you’re attending Digital Summit Dallas, visit booth 9 and meet Kinsta’s official representatives:

- [Zac Pollett](https://www.linkedin.com/in/zachary-pollett) – Senior RevOps Analyst
- [Roger Williams](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rogerwilliamsmedia) – Partnerships & Community Manager, North America
- [Megan Hall](https://www.linkedin.com/in/megan-hall-3b96bb132) – AMERS Account Manager
- [Paul Jackse](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pauljackse) – Head of Sales Development

![The Kinsta team at Digital Summit Dallas](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/digital-summit-dallas-team-announcement-light.png)

The Kinsta team at Digital Summit Dallas

Our team will be on-site, ready to answer your questions, explain how [Kinsta’s managed hosting for WordPress](https://kinsta.com/) can elevate performance, and share insights tailored to your business needs. While chatting with our reps, don’t forget to grab some exclusive Kinsta swag while supplies last.

Make sure to catch **Roger’s talk “Stop Chasing AI Tricks. Start Fixing Your Foundation” on Thursday, 4 December 2025, 11:15 – 12:00**. In this [session](https://www.digitalsummit.com/dallas/speakers), he’ll explain the key hosting fundamentals to improve site performance, without needing deep engineering experience. Join Roger and learn about host evaluation, TTFB, caching layers, HTTP/3, PHP versions, CDN configurations, uptime policies, and more.
