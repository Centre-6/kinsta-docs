A common misconception about web hosting is that it is an expense for agencies and website owners. However, the truth is quite different. Choosing premium hosting is a real investment, rather than a passive item for an agency or online business.

Kinsta hosting is a perfect fit for this, as it provides powerful WordPress development and maintenance tools, many automation features, and top-notch technical support. These features and tools make managing WordPress sites easy, allowing agencies and businesses to allocate fewer resources to tedious maintenance tasks, such as updates and backups. This frees up time for them to focus on their core business and the success of their clients.

Kinsta hosting is also an investment for another reason. Agencies, independent professionals, and tech content creators can monetize their hosting plans by reselling Kinsta hosting, creating a steady source of passive income that can fuel their success and growth.

Let’s explore the different ways to make Kinsta hosting a real bargain.

## Strategy 1: Resell Kinsta hosting

In hosting reselling, you purchase resources and services from a hosting provider and then resell them in smaller, customized packages to your customers. Often, the reseller combines basic hosting with their own services to create a more complete and personalized final product/service.

Hosting reselling is suitable for a variety of online businesses. These range from digital agencies and web designers to front-end developers and IT consultants. Hosting reselling is also popular among hosting companies that do not want to invest in their own infrastructure, such as servers, data centers, and hardware. These businesses rely on providers that already have their own established infrastructure in place.

![Anchor Hosting tech stack](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/anchor-hosting-tech-stack.png)

Anchor Hosting is a perfect example of a reselling strategy (Image source: [Anchor Hosting](https://anchor.host/tech-stack/))

There are several noteworthy advantages for those who choose to resell hosting.

First and foremost, **a hosting reselling strategy does not require significant initial investments**. Resellers do not need to purchase or manage their own servers or hardware infrastructure. There is also less need for staff with technical hardware and system management skills.

**Hosting reselling has the potential to generate considerable income** by charging customers more than the average cost of individual plans.

Additionally, resellers can include services such as website design and maintenance for their customers. This approach is ideal for agencies and freelancers looking to transition from project-based work with inconsistent and unpredictable income to a regular subscription service with a predictable and consistent monthly fee.

Reselling hosting allows agencies and freelancers to **focus on their customers’ specific needs**, providing highly personalized support and marketing services while delegating the challenging tasks of infrastructure maintenance, uptime monitoring, security checks, and more to the hosting provider.

Reselling hosting is particularly advantageous for Kinsta customers because Kinsta provides a comprehensive hosting service package. Those who sign up for a Kinsta hosting plan are getting much more than just hosting, as the package includes a variety of additional tools, features, and services that streamline site management.

Kinsta customers get all of the following included in their hosting plan: [Cloudflare Enterprise CDN](https://kinsta.com/cloudflare-integration/) with 300+ PoPs worldwide, [Cloudflare WAF](https://kinsta.com/blog/cloudflare-integration/), [Edge Caching](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/caching/edge-caching/), automatic daily backups, automatic WordPress updates, a [native API](https://kinsta.com/docs/kinsta-api/) for integration with third-party services, [award-winning](https://kinsta.com/blog/g2-awards/) [premium support](https://kinsta.com/docs/support/contact-support/), an [APM tool](https://kinsta.com/apm-tool/) developed in-house, and much more. All of these features are included in all [hosting plans](https://kinsta.com/pricing/), starting with single-site plans.

Additionally, customers with more specific requirements can upgrade their service package with a variety of [add-ons](https://kinsta.com/add-ons/), including [Kinsta Automatic Updates](https://kinsta.com/add-ons/kinsta-automatic-updates/), [Premium Staging environments](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/staging-environment/#standard-vs-premium-staging), [external backups](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-backups/#external-backups-add-on), enhanced [PHP performance](https://kinsta.com/docs/wordpress-hosting/wordpress-add-ons/#php-performance-add-on), and much more.

Our agency and developer clients adopt different approaches to reselling.

The typical approach is to keep your clients’ sites in your company account and charge them for hosting.

The average cost of each site added to an Agency plan is lower than the cost that a customer would incur when buying a standard plan. For example, the price of a Single 35k annual plan is $30 per month. For an agency with an Agency 60 plan costing $563 per month, however, the average cost of each site decreases.

**Average Total Cost (ATC) = Total cost/Number of sites = $563/n**

If you have 40 sites on your agency account, the ATC is around $14 per month. However, if you have 60 sites, the average cost per site is around $9.38 per month. By reselling hosting at a monthly cost of $35 per site, the agency makes a profit of over $25 per site.

In addition to the hosting itself, the agency may add extra services, such as backup management, site updates (core, plugins, and themes), uptime monitoring, security checks, performance monitoring, and technical support.

Creating a website or web app is no longer enough to succeed in modern markets. By becoming a hosting reseller, you can optimize your efforts and work on a larger scale with multiple clients. Advantages include customer loyalty, constant and predictable cash flows, the ability to evaluate investments in advance, and plan for [long-term growth](https://kinsta.com/blog/reduce-maintenance-workload-client-churn/).

Therefore, reselling hosting may become a key factor in the success and growth of your business.
