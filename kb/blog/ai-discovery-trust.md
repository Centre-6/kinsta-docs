*What consumers actually do after an AI recommendation, according to new Kinsta survey data*

AI is changing how consumers discover products and brands, but this new landscape hasn’t replaced the need for a trusted website.

Kinsta’s survey of 1,008 US consumers shows that consumers use AI tools during their research phase, but they remain skeptical of AI-generated content and often return to a company’s website to verify what they’ve found before making a decision.

## Discovery

- 59.2% use AI tools often or occasionally for research
- 53.8% sometimes or often skip websites entirely during that research
- 61.2% believe AI will eventually replace websites as the primary way people discover products

Among consumers who often skip websites and rely on AI answers:

- 52.2% say AI is faster than searching multiple websites
- 45.2% say it makes it easier to ask follow-up questions
- 42% point to too many ads or pop-ups on websites
- 37.6% say traditional search results feel cluttered or SEO-driven

## Trust in AI

- 58% trust content less once they suspect it was AI-generated
- 52.1% trust traditional search results more than AI-generated answers
- Only 18.8% always trust an AI product recommendation without doing additional research
- 90.7% are concerned about AI-generated misinformation

Consumers also believe AI-generated content is already widespread:

- 86.2% think they encounter AI-generated content often or occasionally online
- 77.3% are very or somewhat confident they can identify AI-generated content

## Websites as verification

After receiving an AI recommendation:

- 44.7% always or most of the time visit a company’s website after receiving an AI recommendation
- 25% visit the company’s website occasionally

Website quality also affects how consumers perceive the company behind it:

- 61.1% associate the quality of a company’s website with the quality of its products or services

## Reviews and authenticity

- 70.9% have suspected a product review was fake or AI-generated
- 59% have stopped engaging with content because they suspected it was AI-generated
- 43.3% say knowing bots are active online makes them trust websites less
