We’re excited to announce that Kinsta is heading back to Australia this May! From **May 11 to 14**, our team will participate in, sponsor, and host several events across **Melbourne and Sydney**, connecting with digital marketers, agencies, publishers, technology leaders, and WordPress professionals.

Kinsta will be represented by two members of our marketing team:

- [**Alex Ando-Michaelson**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alexandermichaelson/) – Partnerships & Community Manager APAC
- [**Sam Gooch**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/samuelgooch/) – Head of Performance Marketing

![Kinsta team at Digital Marketers Australia](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/DigitalMarketers-team-announcement-dark.png)

Kinsta team at Digital Marketers Australia

Alex and Sam will be on the ground throughout the week, meeting with attendees, answering questions, and exploring new ways Kinsta can support Australia’s digital, publishing, agency, and WordPress communities.

## Join us at Digital Marketers Australia in Melbourne

We’re kicking off our schedule at [**Digital Marketers Australia**](https://www.digitalmarketersaustralia.com/), taking place on **May 11-12** at the Amora Herencia Riverwalk Melbourne. The event brings together top digital marketers, agencies, ecommerce teams, enterprise marketers, business owners, and growth-focused professionals for two days of learning and networking.

Stop by the Kinsta-sponsored coffee cart, grab a fresh coffee between sessions, meet Alex and Sam, and chat with us about site speed, performance, hosting infrastructure, and how Kinsta helps teams deliver reliable digital experiences.

Interested in attending? Reach out to the Kinsta team, we’d be happy to share a discount code for registration.

## Be our guest at Kinsta’s Social networking dinners in Melbourne and Sydney

On **May 12 and 14,** we’ll host two invite-only agency and enterprise networking dinners in Melbourne and Sydney.

The Social is an agency- and enterprise-focused business networking event, bringing together digital, marketing, and technology leaders for an evening of connection and collaboration.

![Networking dinner in Melbourne](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/melbourne-social.png)

Networking dinner in Melbourne

After successful editions in Tokyo, Singapore, and Mumbai, The Social is landing in Melbourne for the first time and returning to Sydney to connect industry leaders and explore how global and regional digital teams are driving innovation across WordPress and the open web.

These dinners are designed to create space for meaningful conversations in a relaxed setting, featuring great food, drinks, and opportunities to connect with professionals shaping the future of digital experiences.

If you’re interested in joining us, feel free to reach out to the Kinsta team to request an invitation.

## Let’s meet in Melbourne and Sydney

We’re looking forward to a busy week of conversations with Australia’s digital marketing, agency, and WordPress communities.

Whether you’re attending Digital Marketers Australia, joining one of our private dinners, or meeting us during the week in Melbourne or Sydney, we’d love to connect.

Come say hi to Alex and Sam, learn more about [**Kinsta’s managed hosting for WordPress**](https://kinsta.com/wordpress-hosting/), and explore how we can support your next project, campaign, or client build.

See you in Australia!
