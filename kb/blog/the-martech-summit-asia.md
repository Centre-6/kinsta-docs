We’re thrilled to share that Kinsta will be joining **[The MarTech Summit Asia](https://themartechsummit.com/singapore) in Singapore on March 10-11** at Raffles City Convention Centre!

We’re excited to be back in one of Asia’s most innovative and forward-thinking tech hubs. As one of the leading MarTech events in the region, The MarTech Summit brings together senior marketers, industry leaders, and decision-makers for two days of keynotes, panel discussions, and high-value networking, all focused on shaping the future of marketing.

We’re especially proud to be part of this year’s summit, as The MarTech Summit is also a client and partner of Kinsta. Our relationship began when we sponsored one of their earlier events, giving their team first-hand experience of working with us. You can read how our managed hosting solutions helped them eliminate downtime and stay reliable during traffic surges in [our case study](https://kinsta.com/case-studies/themartechsummit/).

Get ready for two action-packed days filled with insightful sessions and meaningful conversations. This year’s theme, “**Future of MarTech: Unified Foundations, Adaptive Growth**,” explores how organizations can build scalable marketing infrastructures while remaining agile in an increasingly data-driven world.

For a complete breakdown of all sessions and activities, check out the [full agenda](https://themartechsummit.com/singapore-full-agenda). And in between panels, don’t miss the exhibition area where you’ll find Kinsta keeping the conversations flowing. Just follow the unmistakable aroma of freshly brewed coffee, as we’re proudly sponsoring the coffee cart this year!

Swing by for a delicious cup of coffee and exclusive, fun swag, or say hi to our team. We’d love to chat about how [Kinsta’s high-performance hosting solutions](https://kinsta.com/wordpress-hosting/) help marketing teams, agencies, and enterprises build fast, secure, and scalable sites that support measurable growth.

To keep the conversations going beyond the summit, we’re hosting an agency and enterprise business-focused, **invite-only networking event on the 12th of March** for an evening of connection and collaboration. Reach out to our team and let us know if you’re interested in joining us.

## See you in Singapore

We’re looking forward to connecting with the local community in Singapore! If you’re attending the summit, make sure to swing by, enjoy a barista coffee on us, snap a photo with the team, and explore how partnering with Kinsta can power your next stage of growth.

We can’t wait to see you there!
