# Meet Kinsta at WordCamp Asia 2026

We’re excited to share that Kinsta is heading to [**WordCamp Asia 2026**](https://asia.wordcamp.org/2026/), taking place **April 9-11** at the **Jio World Convention Centre in Mumbai, India**. The event will bring together web agencies, developers, marketers, and digital professionals from across the region and beyond.

This year’s event brings the WordPress community to one of the world’s most dynamic and fast-moving cities. From its energy, creativity, and rich culture to its growing digital and tech ecosystem, Mumbai is an exciting setting for WordCamp Asia.

It’s also Kinsta’s first time in India, which makes this event even more special for us. We can’t wait to meet the WordPress community in India, connect with familiar and new faces alike, and be part of the conversations shaping the future of the open web.

## Catch our team at our booth and on stage

If you’re attending WordCamp Asia, be sure to visit the Kinsta booth (**#409**) to meet the team, pick up some exclusive swag, and chat about how we can support your WordPress projects.

Kinsta will have two team members representing the company:

- [**Alexander Ando-Michaelson**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alexandermichaelson/) – Partnerships & Community Manager APAC
- [**Sachi Jain**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sachi-jain-805432a9/) – Account Executive EMEA

![The Kinsta team at WordCamp Asia 2026](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/wcAsia-team-announcement-dark.png)

The Kinsta team at WordCamp Asia 2026

We’re also proud to share that **Alex Ando-Michaelson, Partnerships & Community Manager for APAC** will be moderating a panel discussion on “*How AI is Restructuring Traditional and Modern Marketing Methods.*” The conversation will explore how AI is reshaping SEO, content, and discoverability, as well as the broader shifts taking place across modern marketing strategies.

The panel will also look at how marketing teams and workflows are evolving with AI, along with real examples of what businesses and marketers have recently changed or adapted in response to this fast-moving landscape. For a complete breakdown of all sessions and activities, check out the full agenda [here](https://asia.wordcamp.org/2026/schedule/).

Whether you’re building with WordPress, scaling digital experiences, or exploring how AI is influencing the future of marketing, we’d love to connect and exchange ideas.

## Join us for dinner

To make our time in India even more memorable, we’re also hosting an invite-only networking dinner for digital, marketing, and technology leaders on **Thursday, April 9,** near the WordCamp conference venue.

![Networking dinner hosted by Kinsta in Mumbai](https://kinsta.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/WC-Asia_Mumbai-Social.jpg)

Networking dinner hosted by Kinsta in Mumbai

After successful editions in Singapore, Sydney, and Tokyo, we’re excited to bring this event to Mumbai. It’s a great opportunity to enjoy great food, drinks, and meaningful connections in a relaxed setting with professionals shaping the future of WordPress.

If you’re interested in joining us, feel free to reach out to the Kinsta team to reserve your seat.

## Let’s connect in Mumbai

We’re thrilled to be part of WordCamp Asia 2026 and can’t wait to connect with the incredible WordPress community in India. Come meet us at the booth, attend Alex’s panel, and discover how [Kinsta’s high-performance hosting platform](https://kinsta.com/wordpress-hosting/) helps agencies and businesses build fast, secure, and scalable WordPress experiences.

See you in Mumbai!
