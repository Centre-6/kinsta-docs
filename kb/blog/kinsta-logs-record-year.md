*The company that G2 just ranked #1 across all web hosting is evolving beyond managed WordPress into a full-stack platform for developers, agencies, and enterprise teams*

**LOS ANGELES — March** **31,** **2026 —** Kinsta, a leading managed hosting provider for WordPress, today announced milestones in a record year for the company. Building on its recent recognition as the #1 web hosting platform in G2’s 2026 [Best Software Awards](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260219459095/en/Kinsta-Awarded-1-Web-Hosting-Provider-in-G2s-2026-Best-Software-Awards), Kinsta saw more than 60 percent customer growth year-over-year, made two senior executive hires, expanded its partner programs, and made significant platform improvements for agencies and enterprise teams managing WordPress at scale.

Kinsta added 65,000 new sites to the platform in the past 12 months, bringing its total customer base to over 230,000 across 128 countries. Existing customers include LoudCloud by Barnes & Noble, The Jane Goodall Institute, McDonald’s, TripAdvisor, University of Michigan, Calm, ClickUp, NASA, and Konica Minolta.

“Web traffic is changing quickly, while WordPress businesses have become more sophisticated and more demanding,” said Jon Penland, CEO of Kinsta. “Our growth shows a market that needs hosts that are designed to handle today’s technology landscape. We built Kinsta to be the platform that serious teams choose when they’ve outgrown everything else, and customers are telling us it’s working.”

Eric Zuckerman joins as Chief Financial Officer, with more than 14 years of experience guiding private equity- and venture-backed technology companies through growth and transformation. He most recently was CFO at Datasembly and previously held finance leadership roles at Appspace.

The addition of Eric Zuckerman follows that of Chief Marketing Officer Matt Reid, who [joined](https://kinsta.com/blog/kinsta-names-jon-penland-new-ceo-and-matt-reid-as-cmo/) Kinsta in June 2025 from his previous role as senior vice president at BigCommerce.

“The rest of the industry is focusing on impersonal bots and frustrating self-service, but we’ve doubled down on the human element with our WordPress experts,” said Reid. “We emphasize customer service because that is what makes a difference for our customers in their everyday business. That decision shows up in our retention numbers, our referrals, and ultimately in this growth.”

Kinsta has revamped its Agency Partner Program and Affiliate Partner Program, with a three-tier [program](https://kinsta.com/agency-partner-program/) that aligns participation and performance. Agency partners earn commissions, referrals and co-marketing collaboration scaled to their tier. Affiliates earn lifetime commissions with access to Kinsta resources and co-marketing support based on tiers.

“We moved to Kinsta in 2025 and it’s been a night and day difference from our last host,” said Vin Thomas, founder at [Fixel](https://wearefixel.com/). “Kinsta’s support team is always available the moment we need them. Beyond that, the platform just works. Staging, updates, and day-to-day management is all easier and faster. This saves us time and keeps our clients happy. Hosting has become one less thing we have to worry about, which gives us confidence that our client sites are in good hands.”

Over the past year, Kinsta has also heavily invested in products to help owners and developers running WordPress at scale. Many agency customers, for example, manage dozens or hundreds of client sites and have teams with strict access control and security requirements. To address these enterprise demands, these upgrades aim to eliminate the credential management, manual workflows, limited data visibility and tool switching that result in wasted resources, IT overhead and security risk.

## Key additions in the past 12 months:

**API expansion:** New endpoints cover site resets, domain verification, DNS record management, plugin and theme inventory, WP-CLI command execution, usage data, phpMyAdmin access, activity log retrieval and SSH/SFTP configuration management.

**SSO:** SAML SSO support via Okta and other providers; one-click phpMyAdmin and WP-Admin access from within the Kinsta dashboard.

**Bandwidth-based pricing:** Launched in [November 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251103501509/en/Kinsta-Launches-Bandwidth-Based-Pricing-to-Give-Website-Owners-and-Developers-More-Hosting-Control), gives customers a choice between visit-based and bandwidth-based plans and the ability to easily switch between them, addressing the growing share of bot and AI-generated traffic.
